# Interjections  

Interjections are short utterances that are spoken when a person is very surprised, happy or upset. Interjections are not always whole sentences. Sometimes they are more like a noise than a word.  
For example,  
- Ugh! **(disgust)**  
- Wow! **(amazement)**  
- Phew! **(relief)**  
- Huh! **(surprise)**

## Exercise C  

**Rohan:** **Hi**, Aarav! Is that really you after ten whole years?  
**Aarav:** **Hello**, Rohan! **Wow**, I can't believe my eyes! It's so good to see you!  
**Rohan:** **Well**, look at you! You've grown so tall. When did you move back to town?  
**Aarav:** Just last week! My father got transferred back here. **Hurray**, I'm finally back in our hometown!  
**Rohan:** That's fantastic news! I heard you won a national level chess tournament last year. **Congratulations**, my friend!  
**Aarav:** Thank you so much! **Phew**, it was a tough competition, but all the hard work paid off. So, tell me, how have you been?  
**Rohan:** Oh, I've been doing well. We really missed playing football together in the evenings.  
**Aarav:** **Mmm**, I missed those golden days too! Let's catch up over coffee this weekend and relive old memories.  
**Rohan:** **Awesome**! I'll see you on Saturday then!
