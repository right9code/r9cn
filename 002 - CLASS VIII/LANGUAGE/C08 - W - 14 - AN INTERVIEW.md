# An Interview  

**Read this conversation between a student, Priyanka, and a journalist. Priyanka is being interviewed by the journalist on the activities of her school's nature club, and the recent 'Green Drive' launched by the club.**  

**Journalist:** May I speak to the president of your Nature Club?  
**Priyanka:** I'm the president of the club. How can I help you?  
**Journalist:** I'm Ratan Halder, on behalf of *The Teens*, the bimonthly English magazine. May I know who I'm interviewing?  
**Priyanka:** Hello! I'm Priyanka Dagar. Are you going to print our picture too?  
**Journalist:** We are actually planning a weekly feature on the extra-curricular activities done by the various schools. The week's feature will focus on the nature clubs of different city schools. And yes, there'll be a few pictures.  
**Priyanka:** Oh! I'm so excited! Where should I start?  
**Journalist:** Let me help you. Have you read our magazine?  
**Priyanka:** Sure, I have. It is such a well-presented and well-researched magazine. I think most of us read it.  
**Journalist:** Now, tell me something about the latest activity organised by your club.  
**Priyanka:** Last week, on Republic Day, we planted one hundred saplings in the neighbourhood of our school. We had also invited a few celebrities like Asha Devi, the famous author, and Manmohan Joshi, the well-known painter, to grace the occasion. Oh! It was a great success.  
**Journalist:** How old is your club? What exactly have been the club's activities till now?  
**Priyanka:** Ours is a fairly new club, about four years old. We meet once a week, every Tuesday, after school. Occasionally, we visit the botanical garden to collect specimens. We paste these and also collect data and write about them for future reference. We have four huge scrap books for the specimens. Would you like to take a look at them?  

**Journalist:** Sure, I'd love to. You are also launching a 'Green Drive'. What is it about, and how are you planning to go about it?  
**Priyanka:** We plan to organise drawing, quiz and creative writing competitions, inviting participation from children of the poorer sections of society; we think it will be a good way of spreading awareness about environmental issues, and also of taking the idea of 'Green Drive' to all sections of society.  
**Journalist:** Are there any other activities that you plan to do as part of the 'Green Drive'?  
**Priyanka:** Definitely. We will visit different localities and help the local club members to clean their neighbourhood, plant trees and also teach them to care for them. Recently, we organised a quiz competition. It was a great hit, so we'd like to organise more to spread awareness.  
**Journalist:** How about exchanging ideas with other clubs?  
**Priyanka:** Oh, yes! We're already doing that. We've launched a website and are in touch with quite a few nature clubs both within and outside the country. We're surely trying to spread our wings—let's see how far we can reach!  
**Journalist:** Thank you! This was a delightful interview, and all the best. Now, let me take a picture of some of you with your exceptionally well-made scrap book in front.  
**Priyanka:** Thank you! It was a pleasure talking to you as well.

## Exercise  

1. **Interview with a 12-Year-Old Hero**  

**Reporter:** Hello, Kabir! I'm Sourav from *Young Stars*, the monthly children's magazine. First of all, congratulations on your incredible bravery! How are you feeling today?  
**Kabir:** Hi Sourav! Thank you so much. I'm feeling a bit overwhelmed with all the attention, but I am glad everyone is safe.  
**Reporter:** Our readers are eager to know what happened that afternoon. Could you walk us through the events?  
**Kabir:** Sure! It was around 3:00 PM on Tuesday. I was studying in my room when I heard unusual scratching noises coming from Mr. Kapoor's house next door. I knew Mr. and Mrs. Kapoor had gone to visit their daughter for the week, so the house was supposed to be locked and empty.  
**Reporter:** That was very sharp of you to notice! What did you do next?  
**Kabir:** I peeked through my balcony window and saw a stranger trying to force open their rear glass door with a crowbar. My heart started pounding, but I remembered my father's advice never to panic in a crisis.  
**Reporter:** Did you try to confront him directly, or did you have a plan?  
**Kabir:** I knew I couldn't fight an adult burglar physically. So, I quietly slipped out through our back gate and locked Mr. Kapoor's outer iron security gate from the outside, trapping the intruder in the compound! Then I ran back inside and called the police emergency number 112 as well as our building security guard.  
**Reporter:** That was brilliant presence of mind! How long did it take for help to arrive?  
**Kabir:** The police patrol car arrived within seven minutes! The burglar was trapped inside the yard and had nowhere to run. The officers caught him red-handed with housebreaking tools.  
**Reporter:** Wow, single-handedly trapping a thief at twelve years old! What did your parents and neighbours say?  
**Kabir:** My parents were very proud, though my mother was a little shocked at first. Mr. Kapoor called me from Delhi to thank me personally.  
**Reporter:** You are a true role model for young children everywhere, Kabir. What message would you like to give to our readers?  
**Kabir:** I would tell everyone to stay alert to their surroundings, never panic, and use quick thinking rather than rushing into physical danger.  
**Reporter:** Thank you so much for your time, Kabir, and keep up the great spirit! Let me take a photograph of you with your bravery commendation certificate for our cover page.  
**Kabir:** Thank you, Sourav! It was a pleasure talking to you.  

2. **Interview with the Primary Witness of a Fire Incident**  

**Journalist:** Good evening. I am Riya Sen, senior correspondent for *City Pulse News*. May I ask your name and how you happened to be at the scene when the fire broke out?  
**Witness:** Hello, Ms. Sen. My name is Amit Roy. I live on the third floor of Block B in this apartment complex.  
**Journalist:** Mr. Roy, you played a crucial role by alerting the authorities so promptly. Could you tell us exactly what you saw when the fire started?  
**Witness:** It was around 6:15 PM. I was standing on my balcony having tea when I smelled thick smoke coming from the second floor of Block A across the courtyard. Within seconds, I saw bright orange flames bursting out of the kitchen window of Apartment 204.  
**Journalist:** What was your immediate reaction upon spotting the flames?  
**Witness:** I knew every second counted. I immediately dialed 101 to alert the Fire Services and gave them our exact address and landmark. Right after that, I dialed 100 for the police.  
**Journalist:** That was very swift! Did you also attempt to alert the residents of the building?  
**Witness:** Yes. After hanging up with emergency services, I grabbed a wet towel, ran across to Block A, and pressed the master fire alarm switch on the ground floor. Then I ran up the stairs shouting 'Fire! Evacuate!' to ensure people in adjacent flats evacuated immediately.  
**Journalist:** Were there any people trapped inside Apartment 204?  
**Witness:** Luckily, an elderly lady who lives there was guided out safely by her neighbours just as smoke filled the corridor. Thankfully, everyone got out onto the open lawn before the flames spread further.  
**Journalist:** How quickly did the fire tenders arrive, and how was the fire controlled?  
**Witness:** Three fire engines arrived within twelve minutes. The firefighters acted with extreme efficiency, connecting their hoses and bringing the blaze under control within half an hour.  
**Journalist:** That was remarkable coordination. The chief fire officer mentioned that your prompt phone call prevented a major tragedy. How do you feel about it?  
**Witness:** I am just relieved that nobody was injured. It reminds us all how important it is to keep emergency numbers handy and fire safety equipment functional.  
**Journalist:** Thank you so much, Mr. Roy, for sharing your eyewitness account and for your timely presence of mind.  
**Witness:** You're welcome, Ms. Sen. Stay safe!
