# Paragraph Writing  

A **paragraph** is a piece of writing that consists of several sentences that present and develop one main idea, theme or subject.  

The elements of a paragraph are listed below.  
- **Topic sentence:** This is the first sentence of the paragraph. It introduces the basic idea of the paragraph and motivates the reader to want to read more.  
- **Supporting sentences:** These form the **body** of the paragraph. They prove, explain, develop or support the topic sentence. They give more information about the main idea through examples and strong, convincing points.  
- **Closing sentence:** This restates the topic sentence in different words. It is the last sentence of the paragraph and clearly indicates the end of the paragraph.

# Answers – Paragraph Writing  

## Exercise  

1. **Identification of sentences:**  
   - Topic sentence: My best friend is Gayatri.  
   - First supporting sentence: She lives near my house.  
   - Second supporting sentence: Let me tell you more about her.  
   - Third supporting sentence: We always have fun together.  
   - Closing sentence: I think she is the greatest friend I have ever had.  

   **Cohesive Paragraph:**  
   My best friend is Gayatri, a wonderfully warm and kind-hearted girl. She lives near my house, which makes it extremely convenient for us to meet every single day. Let me tell you more about her, as she has a charming personality and a magnificent sense of humour that brightens everyone's mood. Whether we are cycling in the neighbourhood park, solving complex homework problems, or sharing evening snacks, we always have fun together. Her loyalty and sweet nature make every moment memorable. I think she is the greatest friend I have ever had, and I truly treasure our bond.  

2. **Paragraph based on clues:**  
   Late at night, the ancient city museum stood completely deserted under a pale moon. Driven by sudden suspicious movement, two security guards decided to look into the darkened galleries. As they quietly stepped into the Egyptian section, a chill ran down their spines: the eyes of the monumental Sphinx unexpectedly glowed with a terrifying red light in the total dark. Within moments, two masked burglars scrambled behind the artifact, triggering the security sensors. The loud sound of a siren suddenly echoed through the halls, followed by a sudden crash of shattered glass as the culprits made a narrow escape through a high window. Subsequent police investigation revealed that due to the swift alarm, nothing was found missing.  

3. **Paragraph based on word web (Holidays):**

I wish to go with my family, including my parents, younger brother, and grandparents, because travelling together creates cherished memories. Once there, we will engage in exciting activities such as trekking along green pine trails, visiting the historic Hadimba Temple, and enjoying river rafting in the icy waters of the Beas. What would make this holiday truly special is the breathtaking sight of snow-covered Himalayan peaks, staying in a wooden log cabin near a roaring bonfire, and spending quality, undisturbed time with my loved ones away from the hustle of city life.  

4. **Inside a Whale**  
   During a stormy kayaking expedition off the coast, a colossal blue whale surfaced suddenly and engulfed my kayak in a single giant gulp, carrying me down into its immense stomach. The interior was pitch-black, surprisingly warm, and filled with the overwhelming sound of a giant heartbeat echoing against slimy, pulsating walls of pink tissue. I felt absolute terror and helpless panic as I floated amidst sloshing seawater and half-digested plankton. Expecting the worst, I reached for my pocket flashlight and lit a small waterproof flare. The hot smoke irritated the creature's lining, causing the whale to unleash a massive, violent cough that miraculously expelled me back into the open ocean, where rescue boats picked me up.  

5. **Short paragraphs agreeing or disagreeing with statements:**  

   a. **Teenagers ought to be given more freedom.**  
   I strongly agree that teenagers ought to be given more freedom as they transition into young adults. Granting teenagers autonomy allows them to build self-confidence, make independent decisions, and learn essential life lessons from their own choices. When parents trust them with reasonable freedom, it fosters open communication and mutual respect rather than rebellion. However, this freedom should always come with clear boundaries and accountability. By taking responsibility for their time, studies, and social circles, teenagers develop critical thinking and problem-solving skills necessary for adulthood. Thus, guided freedom is essential for healthy personal growth.  

   b. **Money cannot buy everything.**  
   I completely agree that money cannot buy everything in life. While wealth can purchase material luxuries, comfortable houses, and advanced healthcare, it fails to acquire genuine human values such as true love, sincere friendship, peace of mind, and inner happiness. Respect and good health are built through character, discipline, and emotional bonds, none of which carry a price tag. Furthermore, no amount of money can restore lost time or buy a guilty conscience a peaceful sleep. Ultimately, the most precious aspects of life—contentment, compassion, and cherished memories—are invaluable and entirely beyond the reach of financial transaction.  

   c. **Today's media is a responsible one.**  
   I disagree with the statement that today's media is entirely responsible. While news organizations play a crucial role in disseminating information, modern media is increasingly driven by commercial interests, sensationalism, and clickbait headlines. News channels frequently prioritize high TRPs and dramatic gossip over objective factual reporting, often spreading unverified news and creating unnecessary panic among citizens. Furthermore, partisan bias often distorts public narrative instead of offering neutral viewpoints. Although a few dedicated journalists maintain ethical standards, mainstream media overall needs greater accountability, self-regulation, and truthfulness to truly serve as a responsible fourth pillar of democracy.  

   d. **Technology is killing creativity.**  
   I disagree with the notion that technology is killing creativity. Rather than stifling imagination, modern technological tools provide unprecedented avenues for human expression. Digital software, 3D modelling, digital art platforms, and musical synthesizers enable artists and writers to bring complex ideas to life faster and more vividly than ever before. Access to global digital libraries and creative communities exposes individuals to diverse cultures and innovative concepts. While passive overconsumption of screens can reduce active thinking, technology when used constructively serves as a powerful amplifier of creative potential, democratizing art and empowering creators worldwide.  

6. **Paragraph beginning with prompt:**  
   I jumped. The water was cold and freezing, sending an immediate shockwave through my entire body as I plunged beneath the crystal-clear surface of the mountain lake. For a brief second, the icy chill stole my breath away, but as I kicked hard and surfaced, a thrilling rush of energy surged through my veins. Looking up, I was surrounded by majestic snow-clad peaks reflected impeccably on the turquoise water. The crisp air invigorated my senses, making every paddle exhilarating. Swimming across the frigid lake turned out to be the most refreshing and adventurous experience of our wilderness summer camp.
