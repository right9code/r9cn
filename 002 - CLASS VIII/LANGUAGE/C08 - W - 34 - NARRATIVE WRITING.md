# Narrative Writing  

A **short story** is a powerful form of fiction that can leave a strong impression on the reader even though the writer works with a limited number of words. There are no set guidelines to being a successful writer, but it helps to keep a few points in mind when writing a short story.  

- **Purpose**  
    - to entertain and enthral
- **Structure**  
    - a lively opening that will engage the reader
    - clear story structure—main characters and setting
    - a complication/turning points
    - resulting events
    - resolution of the complication
    - ending has to be linked with the opening
- **Language features**  
    - written in first person or third person
    - written in past tense
    - ideas organised into proper, clear sentences
    - a variety of sentence structures (direct speech, passive voice, simple, compound and complex sentences, etc.) to maintain the reader's interest
    - largely chronological but may contain flashbacks
    - distinctive characters

## Exercise  

1. **Blood Is Thicker Than Water**  

Arjun and Karan were brothers separated by years of bitter business rivalry. Though raised in the same modest house, their differing ambitions led to a painful rift. They built competing textile firms in the city and had not spoken a word to each other in five years.  

One stormy night, a massive electrical short-circuit triggered a raging fire in Karan's warehouse. As alarm bells chimed across the industrial park, workers scrambled out, but Karan remained trapped on the upper floor while trying to retrieve critical financial ledgers.  

News of the blaze reached Arjun at his home. Ignoring his manager's advice to stay clear, Arjun sprinted into the rain and drove frantically to the site. Overcoming thick smoke and falling timber, Arjun wrapped a wet coat around himself, forced open the emergency door, and dragged his unconscious brother out to safety just moments before the roof collapsed. When Karan opened his eyes in the ambulance and saw his brother weeping beside him, all former grudges evaporated into tears of reconciliation. Family bonds, they realized, ran deeper than any worldly dispute.  

2. **Bravery Knows No Gender**  

The snowstorm over the Kumaon hills had grounded all civilian aircraft. A group of four trekkers were stranded at twelve thousand feet, suffering from severe frostbite, and time was running out. Two senior male pilots had deemed an airlift mission impossible due to violent tailwinds.  

Flight Lieutenant Sunita Sharma stepped forward without hesitation. 'I know the valley terrain like the back of my hand, Chief. Give me ten minutes,' she urged her commanding officer. Despite skepticism from her peers, she maneuvered her rescue chopper through dense fog and turbulent air currents with extraordinary precision.  

Reaching the narrow ridge, she held the helicopter in a steady hover inches above the snowbanks. Within twenty minutes, all four trekkers were hoisted safely into the cabin. As the chopper touched down safely at the base hospital, the chief officer saluted Sunita respectfully. She had proved beyond a shadow of a doubt that courage and skill know no gender boundaries.  

3. **An Unexpected Sacrifice**  

'I never expected this from you...' Rohan whispered, staring at his best friend Sameer in disbelief.  

The computer laboratory room was quiet, save for the hum of the cooling fan. Ten minutes earlier, a rare electronic testing kit worth thousands of rupees had slipped from Rohan's nervous hands, shattering on the tiled floor. Panic had seized Rohan, for a prior warning meant he faced immediate expulsion from school.  

Just as the principal entered the room demanded an explanation, Sameer stepped forward calmly and took full responsibility for the accident. He accepted the penalty and community service without uttering a single word of complaint. Later, under the shade of the old banyan tree, Rohan confronted Sameer, asking why he had ruined his own spotless record. Sameer smiled gently and replied, 'You have a scholarship to protect, Rohan. Friends stand by each other when it matters most.' Tears filled Rohan's eyes as he embraced his true friend.  

4. **Guarding the Precious Cargo**  

Ramdeen, an old homeless man, lived near the bustling railway station, accompanied everywhere by his faithful stray dog, Moti. Despite having very little, Ramdeen shared his daily bread with Moti, and the two shared an unbreakable bond of loyalty.  

One chilly winter evening, as dusk settled over the platform, Moti began barking frantically near a pile of unattended cardboard boxes behind a tea stall. Ramdeen hurried over and was astonished to find a sleeping baby wrapped tightly in a yellow shawl, abandoned amidst the cold wind.  

While Moti stood guard over the infant, growling fiercely to deter stray animals and curious onlookers, Ramdeen ran straight to the railway police post. The officers rushed to the spot, wrapped the baby in a blanket, and called an ambulance. Thanks to Ramdeen's swift action and Moti's vigilant protection, the baby was rescued safely and placed in the care of a loving adoption home.  

5. **Winds of Change**  

The small riverside village of Palaspur had long been famous for its handcrafted clay pottery. However, with the advent of cheap factory-made plastic goods, the local artisans faced severe financial hardship, and many young people were abandoning their traditional craft to move to big cities.  

Seventeen-year-old Ananya, daughter of the master potter, refused to let her ancestral heritage perish. She researched modern eco-friendly trends online and persuaded the village elders to experiment with contemporary designs, glazed cookware, and biodegradable clay jewelry. She also set up a social media page to sell the products directly to buyers across the country.  

Within six months, orders began pouring in from major cities. The village workshops buzzed with renewed life, and young artisans returned home to join the thriving cooperative. The winds of change had blown through Palaspur, proving that tradition and innovation together could create a prosperous future.  

6. **A Speech to Remember**  

The school hall was packed for the annual Inter-School Literary Awards. Vikram, a quiet Class VIII student who suffered from a mild stammer, sat nervously in the second row, clutching a crumpled piece of paper. His English teacher, Mrs. D'Souza, had spent months encouraging him to participate in the poetry recitation competition.  

When his name was announced, a quiet hush descended upon the audience. Vikram walked to the podium, took a deep breath, and began to speak. As he recited his original poem about overcoming inner fear, his voice grew steady, resonant, and deeply moving. The raw emotion in his delivery held the entire auditorium spellbound.  

When he finished, the audience erupted into a standing ovation. Moments later, the chief guest presented Vikram with the Gold Trophy for Best Orator. Holding the shining trophy, Vikram walked down the aisle and handed it to Mrs. D'Souza, tears streaming down his cheeks. 'I just didn't know what to say!'  

7. **A New Dawn**  

As the sun rose on the changed landscape, the valley looked entirely unfamiliar. The violent cloudburst of the previous night had triggered a massive landslide, sweeping away the wooden footbridge and altering the course of the mountain river.  

Villagers emerged from the relief shelter, gazing in shock at the mud-caked fields and uprooted trees. However, despair quickly gave way to collective resolve. The village head, Sarpanch Devraj, rallied everyone together on the high ground. 'The land may have changed, but our spirit remains unbroken,' he announced firmly.  

By mid-morning, teams of young men and women were clearing river debris, building a temporary rope bridge, and distributing fresh drinking water to families. Neighbor helped neighbor, sharing food stores and rebuilding collapsed fences. As golden sunlight bathed the valley, the community stepped forward together into a new dawn of resilience.  

8. **The Forbidden Cupboard**  

In the dusty attic of his grandmother's colonial bungalow stood a massive mahogany wardrobe known to all as 'The Forbidden Cupboard'. Grandmother had strictly forbidden anyone from opening its carved brass doors, claiming it contained fragile family heirlooms.  

During a rainy afternoon, twelve-year-old Leo was searching for a lost badminton racket when his foot brushed against a brass key lying under a rug. Driven by overwhelming curiosity, he inserted the key into the cupboard lock. The doors creaked open, revealing stacks of yellowed journals, leather maps, and a silver compass.  

As Leo flipped through the brittle pages of the top diary, he discovered the captivating account of his great-grandfather, who had been a freedom fighter and secret code-breaker during the independence movement. Overwhelmed by pride, Leo realized why his grandmother preserved the cabinet so carefully—it was a sacred shrine to their family's heroic history.  

9. **The Floating City**  

I stepped out of the archway and gasped in wonder. Below me, instead of solid earth, stretched a shimmering sea of pastel clouds dotted with floating crystal towers connected by light bridges. Gravitational laws seemed entirely suspended as people glided effortlessly through the azure sky.  

A winged guide named Zephyr offered me a glowing orb that allowed me to soar above the tallest spire. Together, we swooped past waterfalls cascading into mid-air and gardens where glowing flowers hummed soft melodies. It felt like living inside a breathtaking fantasy beyond human imagination.  

Suddenly, the sky began to flicker with silver lightning, and a deep rumbling sound vibrated through the air, shaking the floating towers. My heart leaped into my throat as I felt myself falling rapidly through the clouds into the vast blue void. And then I woke up.  

10. **Laughter in the Shadows**  

Jojo had spent forty years bringing smiles to crowds as a circus clown. After retiring, he decided to volunteer at St. Jude's Asylum, a residential psychiatric facility, hoping to bring a ray of light to patients suffering from deep trauma and depression.  

Dr. Marcus, the head psychiatrist, was initially skeptical about introducing clowning performance into a medical environment. However, Jojo's gentle demeanor, silent pantomimes, and colorful balloon art slowly broke through barriers that conventional therapies had failed to penetrate. Patients who had not spoken in months began smiling, participating in games, and expressing emotions freely.  

Dr. Marcus watched in amazement during a afternoon session as a deeply withdrawn patient laughed aloud while learning to juggle foam balls with Jojo. Recognizing the profound healing power of joy, Dr. Marcus officially integrated performance arts therapy into the asylum's treatment program, proving that compassion and laughter are potent medicine.
