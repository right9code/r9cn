# Notice Writing  

A **notice** is meant to impart some important information to the public, or to give some warning, or to state a rule. The tone of a notice is formal and impersonal. While framing a notice, one must pay attention to the format as well as the content. The content of a notice must not exceed more than fifty words and all the relevant information must be stated clearly and accurately. Owing to the concise format of a notice, it is important to choose words carefully to ensure that all information is communicated clearly to those concerned.  

**NOTE**:  
Generally, a notice is always marked out of five. The format is marked out of two and three marks are reserved for the content. A notice which exceeds the word limit will be automatically penalised.  

**Read this example of a notice. Pay attention to the format.**  

**Sahitya Samiti** (name of institute)  

**24, D N Shah Road, Allahabad** (address)  

**18 December 2013** (date)  

**Renovation of Library** (heading)  

(body of the notice) The Samiti library will remain closed to the public from 23 December 2013 till 31 December 2013 owing to repair and renovation work. It will reopen on 1 January 2014. The inconvenience to all the members is regretted.  

**J K Joshi** (signature)  
**Secretary** (designation)  

**Now, write notices for the given situations. Remember not to exceed the word limit of fifty words.**  
1. A black-and-white waterproof sports watch has been found in the basketball court on Monday, 23 July 2012. Draft out a notice for the school notice board.  
2. Your school is organising a cultural evening to showcase hidden talents. Write out a notice asking students to audition for the programme.  
3. The basketball match between Martin House and Charnock House has been postponed to a later date for a particular reason. Draft out a notice to inform all concerned.  
4. A fancy dress competition has been organised for the students of Class III. Draft out a notice in not more than fifty words to the parents of the concerned children giving the necessary details.  
5. Your school has decided to contribute warm clothes, blankets and stationery items to the children of an NGO named Calcutta Social Project. Draft out a notice urging students to contribute generously.
