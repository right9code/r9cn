# Picture Composition  

In a picture composition, you use the picture as a reference point and write a composition. Like all compositions, a picture composition, too, should have a beginning, a middle and an end. These are the points you should remember while writing a picture composition.  

- Study the picture carefully and pay attention to all the details such as facial expressions, gestures, clothes and postures of the characters in the picture.  
- Your composition must have a basic plot, characters, dialogues and descriptions on the basis of the theme of the picture.  
- Always write in the past tense.  
- Remember to give your composition a title.

1. **The Rescue at Sea**  

The gale howled furiously across the dark, churning waters of the Bay of Bengal, tossing the small fishing vessel like a wooden toy. Captain Thomas gripped the slippery steering wheel, his eyes strained against the torrents of rain. Beside him, young Rahul clung to the wooden rail, his knuckles white with fear.  

Suddenly, a blinding flash of lightning illuminated the turbulent waves, revealing a overturned raft carrying two stranded fishermen clinging desperately to its side. 'Look, Captain! Over there!' Rahul screamed above the roar of the thunder.  

Without a second thought, Captain Thomas altered course into the ferocious swell. 'Rahul, throw the lifebuoy and secure the winch!' he bellowed. Rahul moved swiftly, braving the treacherous deck. With remarkable precision, he cast the heavy rope towards the struggling men. One by one, the exhausted fishermen were pulled aboard to safety. As the ship turned back toward the harbor, warmth and relief filled the cabin, leaving an indelible lesson of courage and camaraderie in Rahul's heart.  

2. **The Lantern of Hope**  

In a tranquil village nestled along the foothills of the Himalayas, evening was settling gently over the cobblestone lanes. Ten-year-old Meera walked beside her grandfather as he lit the brass lanterns hanging outside their modest cottage. The air was crisp, carrying the fragrant scent of pine trees and blooming night jasmine.  

An old beggar sat quietly on the stone steps near the village square, shivering against the autumn chill. Meera observed his frail frame and hurried back into the house. She returned carrying a thick woolen shawl and a warm bowl of freshly cooked soup.  

'Here, Baba, please take this,' Meera said softly, wrapping the shawl around his shoulders. The old man looked up, his weathered eyes brimming with gratitude. 'May light always guide your path, child,' he whispered. As the village lanterns glowed brightly against the darkening sky, Meera realized that the warmth of compassion was far greater than any physical lamp.  

3. **Tales by the Fireside**  

The crackling fire in the stone hearth threw long, dancing shadows across the cozy wooden cabin. Outside, a thick blanket of snow covered the pine trees of the valley. Wrapped in cozy blankets, young Aarav and his sister Diya sat mesmerized at the feet of their grandfather, Dada-ji.  

Dada-ji held an ancient, leather-bound map in his hands, his eyes sparkling with nostalgia. 'Many decades ago, when I traversed these very mountains,' Dada-ji began in his rich, soothing voice, 'I encountered a valley where forgotten stories came alive at dusk.'  

Aarav leaned forward, spellbound by every word, while Diya asked eagerly about the mythical creatures of the forest. The room was filled with laughter, awe, and the timeless warmth of family bonding. The night wore on, but the magic of Dada-ji's storytelling rendered the cold winter night unforgettable.  

4. **The Whispering Forest**  

Deep within the ancient reserve forest of Bandhavgarh, tall teak trees formed a dense canopy that filtered the morning sunlight into shimmering emerald beams. A group of school friends—Vikram, Priya, and Kabir—were on an environmental nature trail, guided by the local forest ranger, Uncle Ratan.  

As they trekked along the winding soil path, Uncle Ratan stopped and raised his hand, signaling silence. A few paces ahead, a magnificent spotted deer stood gracefully beside a natural spring, drinking quietly. Suddenly, the warning call of a langur echoed through the high branches, warning the forest dwellers of a leopard nearby.  

'Observe how every creature plays a vital role in maintaining the balance of nature,' Uncle Ratan whispered softly. The children watched in breathless awe as the forest seemed to communicate through subtle signals and rustling leaves. Standing amidst the pristine wilderness, Vikram and his friends swore to protect these natural havens for generations to come.
