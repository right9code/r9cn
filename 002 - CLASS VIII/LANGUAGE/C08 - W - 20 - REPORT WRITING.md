# Report Writing  

Writing a **report** for a newspaper or a magazine is very different from other genres of writing since it is largely factual. When covering an event, it is important to mention all the relevant and important facts without adding one's personal opinions. An article written for a magazine may be an opportunity to express one's opinions, but in a report, the idea is to be precise and to the point while holding the reader's attention through an interesting presentation and appropriate use of language.  

Reports are always written in indirect speech, unless a person is being quoted as an eyewitness or an authority on the subject. The passive voice should be used and there should be no use of the personal pronoun, 'I'. If necessary, the phrase 'the reporter' or the 'the correspondent' may be used. Personal opinions may be given only if specifically asked for. Start a report directly with the topic; no elaborate introduction is required. The language used in a report is matter of fact and not ornamented with similes and metaphors. All the relevant points should be narrated in the proper sequence. An account by an eyewitness should be included.  

**Given below is an example of a report. Note the format.**  

**Strike by Delhi Doctors Called Off (a concise heading)**  
Vinay K Jha (name of the reporter)  

New Delhi (name of the place where the event occurred)  
Wednesday, 24 November (day and date)  

(Body of the report) The impending strike by the doctors in the Capital tomorrow has been called off after the successful talks between the Doctors'  

Association of Delhi and the Minister of State for Health, L K Roy. At the end of the talks, which lasted for more than five hours, the minister agreed to look into the demands of the doctors, which included monitoring of hospital appointments and disbursement of salaries, among other issues. The DAD has both private and government hospital doctors as its members.  

**NOTE**:  
- The word limit of a report is usually 100–150 words.  
- A report is normally marked out of twenty. The division is fifteen marks for the content and five marks for the format. The format includes the heading, name of reporter, date and the use of paragraphs.  
- In a question on report writing, some guidelines will always be given. It is imperative to touch upon all the points and to maintain the sequence of events. Any digression from these rules may result in loss of marks for the format.  

**Write reports for these situations. You may base the reports on the guidelines provided in the brackets.**  
1. Mathematics is taught very differently in your school. Recently, there was an exhibition in your school to display the fun way of teaching Mathematics and its advantages. Write a report on the same in the capacity of head **boy/girl** of your school to be published in the student's section of a well-known newspaper.  
    - (date...venue...name of the exhibition...intention of hosting such an exhibition...a brief description of the display...chief guest...who were the invitees...impact on the visitors...brief interviews of some teachers)  

2. Your school has recently felicitated four personalities who have been awarded the Paramveer Chakra. Imagine yourself to be the reporter for a well-known newspaper who had been invited for this occasion. Prepare a report in about 150 words, to be published in the school section of the paper.  
    - (date...venue...name of the programme...purpose...a brief description of the programme...chief guest...how the personalities were felicitated...impact on the audience...brief interviews of some students and teachers)  

3. 'Images', the reading club of your school, has recently invited a famous author to address and motivate the members of the club. As the president of your club, prepare a report to be sent to the press.  
    - (date...venue...name of the author...a brief history of the author...occasion...organisers...description of the programme...highlights of the speech made by the author...presence of any other important member apart from the author (club advisors, students)...total number of students who attended the programme...their feedback...how the programme concluded)  

4. You are a reporter with a leading newspaper, covering the local city news. You have been asked to cover a rally in the city being organised by a local MLA, preceding the municipal elections in the city. Write a report in not more than 150 words on the same.  
    - (date...venue...turnout at the rally...the highlights of the MLA's speech...reaction of the crowd...any direct quotation that might be relevant)

## Exercise  

1. **Math Magic: Fun with Numbers Exhibition**  
By Rohan Sen (Head Boy)  

Kolkata, Saturday, 28 July 20XX  

An innovative Mathematics exhibition titled 'Math Magic: Fun with Numbers' was hosted on Friday, 27 July 20XX, in the Main Auditorium of St. Paul's School. The primary intention behind hosting this exhibition was to remove the fear of Mathematics among students and demonstrate how conceptual learning through interactive models can make the subject engaging and accessible.  

The exhibition featured over fifty working models, 3D geometric puzzles, origami shape transformations, and mathematical game stations created by middle school students. Dr. S. N. Roy, Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at Calcutta University, inaugurated the event as the Chief Guest. Invitees included students and teachers from fifteen neighbouring schools, as well as parents.  

Visitors were visibly impressed by the hands-on approach. Mrs. A. Banerjee, Senior Math Teacher, remarked, 'When students build tangible models of algebraic identities and geometric theorems, abstract concepts become intuitive and clear.' The event successfully transformed the general perception of Mathematics from a daunting chore to an enjoyable intellectual adventure.  

2. **School Felicitates Param Vir Chakra Heroes**  
By Ananya Das (Special Correspondent)  

New Delhi, Saturday, 28 July 20XX  

Army Public School, Dhaula Kuan, hosted a solemn felicitation ceremony titled 'Salute to Valour' in the school auditorium on Friday, 27 July 20XX, to honor four decorated recipients of the Param Vir Chakra, India's highest military decoration.  

The grand event aimed to inspire youth with stories of supreme sacrifice, patriotism, and dedication to the nation. General M. K. Sharma, Chief of Defence Staff, graced the occasion as Chief Guest. The four war heroes were welcomed with a formal guard of honour, presented with ceremonial mementos, and presented with glowing citations by student representatives.  

The auditorium reverberated with emotional applause as documentary snippets showcasing their courage were screened. A Class VIII student noted, 'Meeting these living legends in person has filled us with immense pride and inspired us to serve our country.' The patriotic musical tribute performed by the school choir brought the memorable evening to an inspiring close.  

3. **Renowned Author Addresses 'Images' Reading Club**  
By Sneha Kapadia (President, Images Reading Club)  

Kolkata, Saturday, 28 July 20XX  

'Images', the reading club of Modern High School, organized a special literary interactive session on Friday, 27 July 20XX, in the School Library Hall. The club was honored to host celebrated children's author Ruskin Bond as the guest speaker.  

The event was organized by student members under the guidance of club advisor Ms. R. Ghosh to mark National Literacy Week. Over two hundred enthusiastic student members attended the session. In his captivating address, Mr. Bond shared anecdotes from his childhood in Dehradun and emphasized that reading develops empathy, imagination, and critical thinking.  

The presence of the School Principal and senior faculty members added prestige to the gathering. During the Q&A session, students eagerly asked questions about story plot creation and character building. Participant Aakash Verma shared, 'Listening to Mr. Bond felt like stepping directly into one of his books.' The event concluded with a vote of thanks, followed by an autograph session and light refreshments.  

4. **Massive MLA Rally Precedes Municipal Polls**  
By Vikramaditya Sen (Staff Reporter)  

Kolkata, Saturday, 28 July 20XX  

A massive political rally was organized on Friday, 27 July 20XX, at Park Circus Maidan by local MLA Shri Rajeshwar Nath, ahead of the upcoming municipal corporation elections.  

The rally witnessed a impressive turnout of over five thousand residents and party supporters carrying banners and flags. Addressing the gathering, the MLA highlighted key developmental achievements of his tenure, promising improved road infrastructure, uninterrupted water supply, and enhanced waste management systems in every municipal ward.  

The crowd responded enthusiastically with cheered slogans. Emphasizing civic progress, Shri Nath declared, 'Our commitment is transparent governance and clean, safe neighbourhoods for every family.' The peaceful event concluded with a vote of confidence from local resident associations, setting a charged tone for the upcoming municipal elections.
