# Essay Writing  

An **essay** is a foray into the imagination; it is a work expressing our opinions, thoughts and understanding of a topic or a theme. Like all works of composition, an essay, too, has a format. The usual format of an essay is that it is divided into paragraphs.  

The opening paragraph of an essay is normally called the **introduction**. The last or final paragraph is called the **conclusion**. In between the introduction and the conclusion, there may be one or more paragraphs that constitute the **body** of the composition. These paragraphs help to develop the thoughts and ideas to be expressed in the composition.  

Let us recapitulate the qualities of a good composition.  
- An essay must have **unity of thought**, that is, it should focus on one definite theme.  
- It should follow an **order** or **sequence** and have a **logical flow**.  
- The **length** of an essay ought to be around three hundred words.  
- The composition should be written in a simple **style**.  
- The composition must reveal the personal feelings and opinions of the writer. Without the **personal touch**, a composition becomes devoid of individuality.  

**NOTE**:  
In order to write a good essay, it helps to spend a few moments writing down the questions that arise in your mind after seeing the topic, and trying to answer them.  
Start the composition with the help of a creative introduction. You may use one of the various methods given below—  
- by setting the scene for the story  
- by plunging straight into action  
- by an interesting or unusual detail  
- by a dialogue  
Make sure you end the essay with a meaningful conclusion. Remember to round off your narration and avoid ending your composition with sentences such as 'In conclusion...', 'It can be seen that...', 'Summing up, I'd like to state...'.

## Exercise A  

### Topic 1: Your City on a Festive Evening  

As dusk descends upon the city on the eve of Diwali, a magical transformation unfolds. The grey concrete structures and bustling streets, usually consumed by the relentless noise of daily traffic, dissolve into a shimmering canvas of light and colour. Every balcony, doorway, and rooftop glows with the warm, golden flicker of earthen *diyas* and intricate strings of fairy lights. The air carries a crisp fragrance of jasmine garlands, burning incense, and sweet delicacies being prepared in neighbourhood kitchens.  

Walking through the market square is an immersive sensory experience. Hawkers line the pavements, their stalls overflowing with vibrant marigold flowers, festive decorations, and colorful *rangoli* powders. Families dressed in their finest traditional attire stroll leisurely, greeting neighbors with joy and exchanged boxes of sweets. Children run through open courtyards with sparklers in hand, tracing glowing arcs against the darkening sky. The distant crackle of firecrackers echoes rhythmically, weaving together with laughter and cheerful chatter.  

The festive evening brings a profound sense of unity and warmth to urban life. Differences dissolve as people open their hearts and homes to share blessings. As the night deepens, the city stands illuminated, radiant with hope, harmony, and unbridled happiness.  

### Topic 2: Recess at School  

The ringing of the recess bell at 11:30 a.m. is undoubtedly the most eagerly awaited moment of the school day. In an instant, the quiet discipline of the classroom gives way to a joyous roar as hundreds of students spill into the corridors and playground. The school environment instantly springs to life with energy, laughter, and high-spirited activity.  

The playground transforms into a chaotic hive of recreation. In one corner, a lively game of football begins spontaneously with an old tennis ball, while near the assembly stage, girls gather in animated circles to share stories and snacks. The canteen line is perpetually buzzing as students rush to get hot snacks. Shared tiffin boxes reveal a colorful array of sandwiches, noodles, and home-cooked treats, fostering a sense of camaraderie that no lesson plan can replicate.  

All too soon, the sharp toll of the bell signals the end of the twenty-minute respite. Dusting off their uniforms, students reluctantly head back to their classrooms, energized and refreshed. Recess is not merely a break from academic rigor; it is the beating heart of school life where lifelong friendships are nurtured.  

### Topic 3: The India of Your Dreams  

The India of my dreams is a sovereign, prosperous, and harmonious nation where every citizen lives with dignity, equality, and peace. It is a land where ancient cultural heritage seamlessly blends with modern technological progress, creating a society that respects its past while courageously forging ahead into the future.  

In this visionary nation, education and healthcare are accessible to every child, regardless of socio-economic background. No child is forced into labour, and no youth is deprived of opportunities to excel. Agriculture is modernized to ensure prosperity for our farmers, while sustainable industries protect our precious natural environment. Diversity of language, religion, and culture remains our greatest strength, celebrated with tolerance and mutual respect.  

Ultimately, the India of my dreams is free from the shackles of poverty, corruption, and social discrimination. It stands as a global beacon of peace, innovation, and humanitarian values, fulfilling the aspirations of every citizen.  

## Exercise B  

### Situation 1: An Alien's Perspective on Earth's Waste  

Hovering in my cloak-shielded vessel above the third planet from the sun, my sensors recorded alarming anomalies. Descending toward a dense urban hub, I anticipated witnessing advanced planetary care from a civilization that calls itself intelligent. Instead, I was profoundly shocked by the overwhelming piles of discarded matter smothering the terrestrial ecosystem.  

Giant mountains of synthetic non-biodegradable polymer—what Earthlings call 'plastic'—choked waterways and smothered once-fertile soil. I observed pristine oceans polluted with discarded containers, trapping marine life in toxic tangles. In city centers, heaps of food waste lay rotting beside glittering glass towers, emitting foul gases while hungry creatures scavenged nearby. The atmosphere was heavy with acrid smoke from burning refuse heaps, suffocating the very air the inhabitants breathe.  

It is terrifying to witness a species squandering its natural sanctuary with such reckless disregard. If Earthlings do not immediately alter their consumption habits and adopt closed-loop recycling, their beautiful planet will soon collapse under the weight of their own waste.  

### Situation 2: Three Wishes Granted  

If a benevolent force were to grant me three wishes today, I would select them with deep reflection, prioritizing global well-being over personal indulgence.  

My first priority wish would be the eradication of all incurable diseases and global pandemics. Health is the foundation of human existence; freeing humanity from physical suffering would restore hope and joy to millions of families across the world.  

My second wish would be the complete restoration of Earth's natural climate and ecosystems. I would wish for polluted rivers to run clear, endangered species to thrive, and forests to flourish once more, reversing environmental damage.  

My final wish would be the universal access to quality education for every child on Earth. Education empowers minds, eradicates prejudice, and equips future generations to solve complex challenges peacefully.  

### Situation 3: Making the World a Happier Place  

Making the world a happier place does not necessarily require grand political movements; it begins with intentional, compassionate actions in our daily lives. To create a happier world, I would focus on fostering empathy, active kindness, and community connection.  

First, I would initiate community support circles in neighborhoods to ensure no elderly or lonely individual feels isolated. Simple acts like listening attentively and offering help build strong emotional bonds. Second, I would promote environmental stewardship by organizing tree-planting drives and neighborhood cleanups, as living in clean, green surroundings directly enhances mental well-being.  

Finally, I would advocate for kindness education in schools, teaching children emotional intelligence, gratitude, and peaceful conflict resolution. When we cultivate compassion in young minds, we plant the seeds for a peaceful, harmonious world.
