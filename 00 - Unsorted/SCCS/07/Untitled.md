**Q1. Who is the speaker/author of the poem "My Dear Soldiers"?**

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>

**Answer:** (B) Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam  
**Explanation:** The poem is composed by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam as a tribute to the soldiers of the nation.  
</details>

**Q2. Which poetic device is used in the opening address, "Oh! Defenders of borders"?**

(B) Apostrophe  
(C) Hyperbole  
(D) Onomatopoeia  

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>

**Q13. What is the poetic effect of repeating the pronouns "You" and "We" throughout the poem?**

(D) It makes the poem sound like an informal conversation

**Q14. The repetition of the word "You" at the beginning of several lines ("You are great sons...", "You still hold on...", "You are there guarding...") is an example of:**  
(A) Anaphora  
(B) Epistrophe  
(C) Oxymoron  
(D) Chiasmus

**Q17. The listing of soldier duties without conjunctions ("Climbing the heights or striding the valleys / Defending the deserts or guarding the marshes") exemplifies which literary device?**  
(A) Polysyndeton  
(B) Asyndeton  
(C) Euphemism  
(D) Paradox  

<details>
<summary>View Answer</summary>

**Answer:** (B) Asyndeton  
**Explanation:** Asyndeton is the omission of conjunctions between phrases or clauses, creating a rapid, continuous rhythm that conveys vast scope.  
</details>
