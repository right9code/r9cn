**Q1. Who is the poet of "Magnifying Glass"?**

(B) Walter de la Mare  
(C) William Wordsworth  
(D) John Keats  

<details>

**Answer:** (B) Walter de la Mare

**Q3. In the first stanza, what does the magnifying glass reveal inside a "scrap of chalk"?**
