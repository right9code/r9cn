## 📛 1. NOUN — *Names a person, place, thing, or idea*

> **Quick test:** Ask "What?" or "Who?" → the answer is a noun.
> `Nainital is a beautiful city.`

### The 5 Kinds

| Type | What it names | Example |
|------|--------------|---------|
| 🏷️ **Proper** | A *particular* person/place/thing (always capitalized) | *Mohit, Taj Mahal, India* |
| 🏪 **Common** | A *general* person/place/thing | *city, boy, mountain* |
| 👥 **Collective** | A *group* treated as one unit | *army, herd, jury, flock* |
| 💭 **Abstract** | A *quality, state, or feeling* (cannot touch) | *honesty, wisdom, childhood* |
| 🪵 **Material** | The *substance* something is made of | *copper, jute, silver* |

### Countable vs Uncountable

| Type | Can be counted? | Takes a/an? | Example |
|------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| ✅ **Countable** | Yes | Yes | *one girl, ten boys, two apples* |
| ❌ **Uncountable** | No | No | *news, tea, salt, information* |

### 🚻 Gender

| Gender | Refers to | Example | Opposite |
|--------|-----------|---------|----------|
| ♂️ Masculine | Male | *hunter, tiger, uncle* | huntress, tigress, aunt |
| ♀️ Feminine | Female | *tigress, aunt, doe* | tiger, uncle, buck |
| ⚪ Neuter | Non-living | *stone, book, table* | — |
| 🔵 Common | Male or female | *student, orphan, doctor* | — |

### 🔢 Number — Singular → Plural Rules

| Rule | Singular | Plural |
|------|----------|--------|
| Add **-s** | garden | gardens |
| Add **-es** (ending -x, -ch, -s, -sh) | tax, dish | taxes, dishes |
| **-y** → **-ies** (after consonant) | duty | duties |
| **-y** → **-ys** (after vowel) | day | days |
| **-f/-fe** → **-ves** | knife, calf | knives, calves |
| Same form | deer, fish | deer, fish |
| ⚠️ Always singular (uncountable) | furniture, advice | — |

## 🔁 2. PRONOUN — *Takes the place of a noun*

> **Quick test:** Replaces a noun so you don't repeat it.
> `Mohit is an athlete. He practises regularly.` (He = Mohit)
> The noun being replaced is called the **antecedent**.

### The 10 Types

| Type | What it does | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| 👤 **Personal** | Refers to a person/thing | *I, you, he, she, it, we, they* |
| 🤲 **Possessive** | Shows ownership (stands alone) | *mine, yours, his, hers, theirs* |
| 👉 **Demonstrative** | Points to specific things | *this, that, these, those* |
| ❓ **Interrogative** | Asks questions | *who, what, which, whom* |
| 🪞 **Reflexive** | Subject acts on itself | *myself, himself, themselves* |
| 💪 **Emphatic** | Adds emphasis (same form as reflexive) | *He **himself** did it.* |
| 🔤 **Indefinite** | Refers to no specific person/thing | *somebody, few, all, none, one* |
| 🔢 **Distributive** | Refers to one at a time (always singular) | *each, either, neither* |
| 🤝 **Reciprocal** | Mutual action between two or more | *each other, one another* |
| 🔗 **Relative** | Introduces a clause referring to a noun | *who, which, that, whose, whom* |

### 🎯 Key Distinctions

> **Reflexive vs Emphatic:** Same words, different jobs.
> - *He cut **himself**.* → Reflexive (he is the object of his own action)
> - *He **himself** cut the cake.* → Emphatic (adds emphasis, can be removed)

> **Reciprocal:** Used when two or more people do the same thing to each other.
> - *The sisters help **each other**.* (Two people)
> - *The team members support **one another**.* (More than two)

## 🎨 3. ADJECTIVE — *Describes or qualifies a noun/pronoun*

> **Quick test:** Ask "What kind?" or "How many?" → the answer is an adjective.
> `He is a brave soldier.`

### The 8 Types

| Type | What it tells | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| 🏅 **Quality** | What kind | *brave soldier, beautiful river* |
| 📏 **Quantity** | How much | *some money, little water, enough food* |
| 🔢 **Number** | How many / what order | *ten apples, first prize, few books* |
| 👉 **Demonstrative** | Points to specific noun | *this book, those mangoes* |
| ❓ **Interrogative** | Asks about a noun | *Which pen? Whose bag?* |
| 🤲 **Possessive** | Shows belonging (before noun) | *my book, their house, her dress* |
| 💪 **Emphatic** | Adds force | *complete success, the very dress* |
| 🔄 **Participial** | Verb form used as adjective | *running stream (present), broken window (past)* |

### 🔢 Number — Subtypes

| Subtype | Meaning | Example |
|---------|---------|---------|
| Cardinal | Exact count | *one, twenty, hundred* |
| Ordinal | Order in series | *first, second, last* |
| Indefinite | Not exact number | *few, many, several* |
| Distributive | One at a time | *every, each, either* |

### 📈 Degrees of Comparison

| Degree | Use | Example |
|--------|-----|---------|
| Positive | No comparison | *good* |
| Comparative | Compare two | *better* |
| Superlative | Compare more than two | *best* |

## ⚡ 4. VERB — *Expresses action, state, or being*

> **Quick test:** Ask "What does it do?" or "What is it?" → the answer is a verb.
> `She designs clothes. The tiger is a magnificent animal.`

### By Transitivity

| Type | Has an object? | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| ➡️ **Transitive** | Yes — action passes to a receiver | *She cut **the cake**.* |
| 🛑 **Intransitive** | No — action stops at the doer | *The child wept.* |

### Direct vs Indirect Object

> Some transitive verbs take **two objects**:
> - **Direct Object** — receives the action directly (ask: verb + *what?*)
> - **Indirect Object** — receives the direct object (ask: *to/for whom?*)

`She gave **me** (indirect) **a book** (direct).`

### By Form

| Type | Changes with subject? | Example |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| ✅ **Finite** | Yes — agrees in person/number/tense | *He writes. They write.* |
| ❌ **Non-finite** | No — stays the same | *to write (infinitive), writing (gerund), written (participle)* |

### 🔄 Reflexive Verbs

> When the subject and object are the same person/thing:
> *He hurt **himself**.* | *She blamed **herself**.*

### 🚫 Verbs That Can NEVER Be Transitive

> These verbs can never take an object:
> *come, go, fall, die, sleep*

## 🔮 5. MODAL — *Expresses the speaker's attitude*

> **Quick test:** A helping verb that shows ability, permission, obligation, possibility...
> `She **can** swim.` | `You **must** stop.`

### The 12 Modals and Their Uses

| Modal | Main Uses | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 🟢 **Can** | Ability, informal permission | *She can swim. Can I go?* |
| 🔵 **Could** | Past ability, polite request | *Could you help me?* |
| 🟡 **May** | Formal permission, wish, weak possibility | *May I come in? It may rain.* |
| 🟠 **Might** | Very weak possibility | *It might rain tonight.* |
| ⚪ **Shall** | Offer/suggestion (1st person), command/promise (2nd/3rd) | *Shall I open the door? You shall be rewarded.* |
| 🔴 **Will** | Certainty, determination, future | *I will help you. The train will leave at 10.* |
| 🟣 **Would** | Polite request, past habit | *Would you lend me a pen? We would sit for hours.* |
| 🔶 **Should** | Advice, suggestion, probability | *You should tell the truth.* |
| 🔷 **Must** | Necessity, strong obligation | *We must do this right away.* |
| 🔸 **Ought to** | Moral obligation, duty | *We ought to obey our parents.* |
| 🔹 **Used to** | Discontinued past habit | *She used to learn yoga.* |
| ⚠️ **Need** | Necessity (mainly questions/negatives) | *Need I say more? You need not go.* |
| ⚡ **Dare** | Boldness, challenge | *How dare you say so?* |

### 🎯 Key Distinction

> **Must** vs **Mustn't** vs **Needn't**
> - *You **must** do it.* → It is necessary that you do it.
> - *You **mustn't** do it.* → It is necessary that you do NOT do it.
> - *You **needn't** do it.* → It is NOT necessary for you to do it.

## 🌀 6. ADVERB — *Modifies a verb, adjective, or another adverb*

> **Quick test:** Ask "How?" "When?" "Where?" "How often?" → the answer is an adverb.
> `Rahim writes neatly.` (How? → neatly)

### The 7 Types

| Type | Question it answers | Example |
|------|-------------------|---------|
| 🕐 **Time** | When? | *yesterday, soon, before* |
| 🔁 **Frequency** | How often? | *always, rarely, twice, never* |
| 📍 **Place** | Where? | *here, outside, downstairs* |
| 🎭 **Manner** | How? | *neatly, bravely, softly* |
| 📊 **Degree** | To what extent? | *very, quite, almost, too* |
| 💡 **Reason** | Why? (for what reason) | *therefore, consequently* |
| ❓ **Interrogative** | Asks a question | *When? Where? How? Why?* |

### 📈 Comparison of Adverbs

> Like adjectives, adverbs have three degrees:

| Degree | Formation | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Positive | Base form | *fast, happily* |
| Comparative | -er or more | *fast**er**, **more** happily* |
| Superlative | -est or most | *fast**est**, **most** happily* |

### 🔧 Formation from Adjectives

| Rule | Adjective | Adverb |
|------|----------|--------|
| Add **-ly** | rude | rudely |
| **-y** → **-ily** (after consonant) | happy | happily |
| **-e** → drop e, add **-ly** | true | truly |
| **-ic** → add **-ally** | basic | basically |

> ⚠️ Some words are the same as adjectives AND adverbs: *fast, hard, early, late, daily*
> - *He is a **fast** runner.* (adjective)
> - *He runs **fast**.* (adverb)

## 📍 7. PREPOSITION — *Shows relation between noun/pronoun and another word*

> **Quick test:** A word before a noun/pronoun showing its relationship to another word.
> `The baby is on the bed.` (on shows relation between baby and bed)

### By Relation

| Type | Shows | Example |
|------|-------|---------|
| 🕐 **Time** | When something happens | *at noon, on Monday, in July* |
| 📍 **Place** | Where something is | *on the table, in the room, at the door* |
| ➡️ **Motion** | Movement toward/away | *into the pool, towards the door, out of the room* |

### By Form

| Type | What it is | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| 🔤 **Simple** | One word | *in, on, at, by, to, for* |
| 🔗 **Compound/Phrase** | Multiple words | *in front of, because of, in place of* |

### 🎯 The Big Three: at / on / in

> These three work for BOTH time AND place:

| Preposition | Time Meaning | Place Meaning |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|
| **at** | Exact point: *at 8 o'clock* | Specific point: *at the door* |
| **on** | Day/date: *on Monday, on 20 October* | Surface: *on the table* |
| **in** | Longer period: *in September, in 2024* | Enclosed space: *in the room* |

## 🔗 8. CONJUNCTION — *Joins words, phrases, or sentences*

> **Quick test:** A word that connects two or more things.
> `I was late but I still managed to finish.`

### 📖 Before You Learn — What is a Clause?

> A **clause** is a group of words that has a **subject** and a **verb**.

| Type | What it is | Can it stand alone? | Example |
|------|-----------|---------------------|---------|
| ✅ **Independent (Main) Clause** | Makes complete sense on its own | Yes ✓ | *She was tired.* |
| ❌ **Dependent (Subordinate) Clause** | Does NOT make complete sense alone; needs a main clause | No ✗ | *because she was tired.* (incomplete!) |

> 💡 **Think of it like this:** An independent clause is like a complete sentence. A dependent clause is like a sentence fragment — it needs to be attached to a main clause to make sense.

### The 3 Types of Conjunctions

| Type | What it joins | How it works | Examples |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| 🤝 **Coordinating** | Two **equal** clauses (both can stand alone) | Joins equal to equal | *and, but, or, nor, yet, so, for* |
| 🔗 **Subordinating** | A **dependent** clause to an **independent** clause | Joins unequal parts (one needs the other) | *because, although, when, if, unless, since, after* |
| 🔀 **Correlative** | Two equal clauses **in pairs** | Always used in pairs | *either…or, neither…nor, both…and, not only…but also* |

### 🎯 Easy Examples

| Type | Sentence | Why? |
|------|----------|------|
| 🤝 Coordinating | I was tired **but** I went to the party. | Both halves can be sentences on their own: *I was tired.* ✓ *I went to the party.* ✓ |
| 🔗 Subordinating | I left **because** I was tired. | *"because I was tired"* alone is NOT a complete sentence ✗ |
| 🔀 Correlative | **Neither** he **nor** I can do this. | *Neither…nor* is a pair joining two equal parts |

### 📋 Subordinating Conjunctions — By Function

| Function | What it shows | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| 🕐 **Time** | When an action happened | *when, before, after, while, until, till, since, as soon as* |
| 📍 **Place** | Where an action happened | *where, wherever* |
| 💡 **Reason** | Why something happened | *because, since, as* |
| 📊 **Result** | One idea is the result of another | *so...that, such...that* |
| 🎯 **Purpose** | The goal of an action | *so that, in order that* |
| 🔀 **Condition** | Under what condition something happens | *if, unless, in case, even if* |
| ⚖️ **Contrast** | Something unexpected | *although, though, even though* |
| 📏 **Comparison** | Comparing two things | *than, as...as* |
| 🎭 **Manner** | How an action takes place | *as, as if, as though* |

### 🔀 Correlative Conjunctions — The Pairs

| Pair | Function | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **either…or** | Choice between two | *Either you leave or I will call the police.* |
| **neither…nor** | Not one and not the other | *Neither he nor I can do this.* |
| **both…and** | Two things together | *Both the teacher and the student were happy.* |
| **not only…but also** | Adding emphasis | *Not only did he help, but also he donated money.* |
| **so…that** | Result/consequence | *He was so tired that he fell asleep immediately.* |
| **such…that** | Result (with noun) | *Such was his condition that we lost hope.* |
| **no sooner…than** | One thing immediately after another | *No sooner had I entered than the phone rang.* |
| **as…as** | Equality comparison | *He is as tall as I am.* |

## 💥 9. INTERJECTION — *Expresses sudden emotion*

> **Quick test:** A word or sound that expresses a feeling — has no grammatical connection to the rest of the sentence.
> `Hurrah! We won the tournament.`

### By Emotion

| Emotion | Interjections | Example |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| 🎉 **Joy** | *Hurrah! Yippee! Ha ha! Wow!* | *Hurrah! We won!* |
| 😢 **Sorrow** | *Alas! Ah! Oh!* | *Alas! He is gone.* |
| 😲 **Surprise** | *Oh! What! Wow! Huh! Good heavens!* | *Oh! What a shock!* |
| 👋 **Attention** | *Listen! Look! Hello! Hey! Behold!* | *Listen! I have something to say.* |
| 👏 **Appreciation** | *Bravo! Well done! Congratulations!* | *Bravo! That was amazing!* |
| 🤫 **Silence** | *Hush! Shh!* | *Hush! The baby is sleeping.* |
| 🤢 **Disgust** | *Ugh! Yuck! Pooh!* | *Ugh! This tastes horrible!* |
| 😣 **Pain** | *Ouch!* | *Ouch! That hurt!* |
| 😌 **Relief** | *Phew!* | *Phew! That was close!* |
| 😬 **Mistake** | *Oops!* | *Oops! I dropped it.* |
| 🤧 **Sneeze** | *Achoo!* | *Achoo! Excuse me.* |
