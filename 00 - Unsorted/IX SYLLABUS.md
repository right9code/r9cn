# English Language & Literature Syllabus and Exam Blueprint  

## Summary Breakdown  

* **Total Marks:** 80
* **Section A (Reading Skills):** 20 Marks
* **Section B (Writing Skills & Grammar):** 30 Marks
* **Section C (Language through Literature):** 30 Marks  

---

## Section A – Reading Skills (20 Marks)  

* **Descriptive / Discursive Passage** (10 Marks | 400–450 words)
* **Case-Based Passage** (10 Marks | 200–250 words) — incorporates visual inputs, charts, or statistical data
* **Combined Length:** 600–700 words
* **Question Formats:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), Objective Type, Very Short Answer Questions (VSAQs), and Short Answer Questions (SAQs)  

---

## Section B – Writing Skills and Grammar (30 Marks)  

### Grammar (10 Marks)  

**Core Syllabus:**
* Sequence of Tenses
* Modal Auxiliaries (ability, obligation, permission, possibility, advice)
* Reported Speech (statements, questions, commands, requests)
* Conditional Clause – Type 1
* Subject–Verb Concord
* Determiners
* Clauses (Noun Clause, Relative Clause)  

**Question Breakdown:**
* **Editing / Omitting** (MCQ format) — 4 Marks
* **Sentence Rearrangement** — 3 Marks
* **Sentence Transformation** — 3 Marks  

### Writing Skills (20 Marks)  

* **Notice OR Informal Invitation** (Up to 50 words) — 3 Marks
* **Letter to Editor OR Formal E-mail** (120–150 words) — 5 Marks
* **Factual Description OR Magazine Article** (120–150 words) — 5 Marks
* **Descriptive OR Narrative Essay** (200–250 words) — 7 Marks  

> **Note:** Each writing question includes an internal choice between two options.  

---

## Section C – Language through Literature (30 Marks)  

### Extract-Based Questions (10 Marks)  

* **Drama / Prose Extract:** Choose 1 out of 2 extracts — 5 Marks
* **Poetry Extract:** Choose 1 out of 2 extracts — 5 Marks
* **Question Formats:** MCQs, Objective Type, and VSAQs  

### Short & Long Answer Questions (20 Marks)  

* **Short Answer Questions:** Answer 5 out of 6 (40–50 words each) — 10 Marks
* **Long Answer Question (Extrapolation):** Choose 1 out of 2 (120–150 words) — 5 Marks
  * Assesses extrapolation beyond and across texts.
* **Long Answer Question (Theme / Plot / Character):** Choose 1 out of 2 (120–150 words) — 5 Marks
  * Assesses core literary components.
