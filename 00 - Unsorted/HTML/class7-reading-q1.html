Section A — Reading Skill

1. Read the passage given below:

Ravi grew up in Sundarpur, a small town surrounded by fields and dusty roads. Life there moved slowly. Farmers walked to their fields at dawn, and in the afternoon the main bazaar grew quiet and sleepy. Most families knew one another, and children played in the lanes until the light faded.

Near Ravi's house, however, there was an empty plot of land that nobody looked after. People often dumped broken furniture and plastic bags there. The place smelled unpleasant, and flies buzzed around it all day. Every time Ravi walked past it, he felt that such a large open space was being wasted.

One Sunday morning, Ravi suggested to his friends that they should clean the plot and turn it into a garden. At first, the idea seemed impossible because the children had no tools and very little money. Some neighbours even laughed at them, saying that a group of schoolchildren could never change a dumping ground.

Instead of giving up, the group decided to ask for help. They borrowed brooms and spades from their neighbours and collected seeds from their own homes. Every Sunday, they met to pull out weeds, level the soil, and plant saplings. They carried water in buckets from a nearby tap and covered the young plants with leaves to protect them from the heat.

Slowly, the barren plot began to change. Green shoots appeared, flowers bloomed, and the air felt fresher. Birds returned to the branches, and butterflies hovered over the beds. Villagers who once ignored the place started visiting it in the evenings to sit and relax.

The children learned that cooperation and patience could transform even a neglected corner into something beautiful. Because it brought peace to everyone, the villagers fondly called the garden Sukoon. Today the garden is looked after by a new group of young volunteers, and the plot that once embarrassed the town has become its favourite meeting place.
