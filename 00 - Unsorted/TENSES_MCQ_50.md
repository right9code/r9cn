**1. Which tense is used to express habitual actions, universal truths, and fixed schedules?**  
A) Present Continuous  
B) Present Indefinite  
C) Present Perfect  
D) Past Indefinite  

<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) Present Indefinite</details>

---

**2. "She eats an orange every day." — Which tense is this?**  
A) Present Continuous  
B) Present Perfect  
C) Present Indefinite  
D) Past Indefinite  

<details><summary>Answer</summary>C) Present Indefinite</details>

---

**3. In Present Indefinite Tense, we add -es to verbs ending in -ch, -sh, -s, -x, or -z. Which is correct?**

B) Wash → Washes  
C) Play → Playes  
D) Fix → Fixs  

<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) Wash → Washes</details>

**4. In Present Indefinite negative sentences, singular subjects use:**  
A) do not + Verb (I)

C) has not + Verb (III)

<details><summary>Answer</summary>B) does not + Verb (I)</details>

**6. "If you do not work hard, you will fail." — The Present Indefinite in the if-clause is used to:**
