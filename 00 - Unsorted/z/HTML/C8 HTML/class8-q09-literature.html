Section C — Literature

Same pattern as the paper: each extract is a short passage from a Class VIII poem, followed by (i) detail, (ii) meaning, (iii) identify the poem and poet, (iv) inference, (v) one word from the extract.

In (iii) you build the answer from jumbled words — tap the words of the poem’s name in order, then the poet’s name in order. Both word sets contain extra words, including other poets’ names, so a right poem with a wrong writer is possible: only one full answer is correct. Tap a filled word to take it back.

The poem’s name stays hidden until you check. Neither of the two extracts printed in the paper is used here.
