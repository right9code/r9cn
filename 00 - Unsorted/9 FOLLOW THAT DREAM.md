## 📌 PART 1: REVISION CAPSULE  

### 1. Key Thematic Insights  

* **Author & Context:** Written by Irene Chua as a letter to her teenage daughter, Ming (from *My Daughter, My Friend*, 19 June 1995).  
* **The 10-Year Mastery Rule:** Achieving world-class skill in any endeavor demands dedicated, singular pursuit for at least a decade.  
* **Intrinsic Fuel:** When external stamina fades, genuine love for the craft and the prospect of eventual success keep one going.  
* **Support Networks:** No victor stands alone; behind every Academy Award winner is an army of mentors, family, and associates.  
* **Evolving Dreams:** Life hurdles (e.g., WWII disruption, family breadwinning) can reshape aspirations; secondary/later dreams (like the mother publishing her book after 10 years) carry equal dignity and value.

#### Q2. The mother advises Ming that before taking the final "plunge", she must `_______`.  
* (A) ensure she has public recognition  
* (B) count the cost in years, finances, and personal sacrifices  
* (C) wait until all potential risks disappear  
* (D) consult critics for approval  

<details>

#### Q3. The reference to Academy Award winners highlights the vital importance of `_______`.
