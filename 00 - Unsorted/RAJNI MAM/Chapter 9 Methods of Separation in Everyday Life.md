#### Short Answer Questions

**1. What is filtration? Give one example.**  
**Ans.** Filtration is used to separate an insoluble solid from a liquid using a filter. The solid particles remain on the filter while the liquid passes through it.  
*Example:* Separating sand from water.

---

**2. What is sedimentation?**  
**Ans.** Sedimentation is the process in which heavier insoluble particles settle down at the bottom of a liquid when it is left undisturbed. It is commonly used for separating mud from water.

---

**3. What is winnowing?**  
**Ans.** Winnowing is a method used to separate lighter substances such as husk from heavier grains with the help of moving air. The lighter husk is carried away by the wind while the heavier grains fall down.

---

#### Long Answer Questions

**1. What is sedimentation? Explain how sedimentation and decantation can be used to separate mud from water.**  
**Ans.** Sedimentation is the process in which heavier insoluble particles settle down at the bottom of a liquid when it is left undisturbed.  

To separate mud from water, the mixture is kept undisturbed for some time so that the mud settles at the bottom. This process is called **sedimentation**. The clear water above it is then carefully poured into another container without disturbing the settled mud. This process is called **decantation**.  

---

**2. Explain any four methods of separation used in everyday life with examples.**  
**Ans.**  
* **Handpicking:** Removing stones or impurity particles from food grains like rice or pulses by hand.  
* **Sieving:** Separating finer flour particles from coarse bran using a mesh strainer/sieve.  
* **Filtration:** Separating tea leaves from tea using a strainer.  
* **Evaporation:** Obtaining salt from seawater by allowing the water to evaporate under the sun.
