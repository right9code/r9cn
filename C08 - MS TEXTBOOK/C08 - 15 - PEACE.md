# Peace  

## Let's Begin  

Once upon a time, there was a king who offered a prize to the artist who would paint the best picture of perfect peace. Many artists from all around came and tried. The king admired all the paintings, but there were only two of them which were near to what he wanted to see. Now he had to choose the winner between them.  

One painting portrayed a beautiful scenery. It had lofty mountains covered with green vegetation and having a lake. The sky in the scenery looked beautiful because of the fluffy white clouds. The tree made the painting more attractive. To add to the peace content, a bird was sitting on the branch of the tree.  

The second painting was that of barren mountains with no vegetation at all. The sky was black and lightning struck the clouds. There was a waterfall on the rocky hillside.  

Just a tiny bird was sitting in the hole in the rocky hill. The bird's beak was open so as to portray that it was singing some song although the surroundings seemed to be unpleasant.  

Now, the king examined the two paintings and finally gave the results. He awarded the prize to the second one. He explained, 'Both these paintings depict peace  

beautifully. But real peace is not the state of rest that one enjoys in silent atmosphere. But perfect peace means one is calm at heart while in the midst of all unavoidable circumstances like noise, troubles, work and disturbances. Everyone was happy to learn the explanation of the king.  

**Moral :** Inner peace determines the nature of perfect peace.  

## Overview  

Peace is the result of training your mind to face life as a challenge. It is basically the spiritual calm despite the presence of stressors in the outside world. Focus your attention on those things you can control and be true to yourself, then only you will find inner peace.
