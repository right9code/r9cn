# Save the Environment  

## Let's Begin  

**Read this poem on saving the earth:**  

Of all the planets, I am special,  
I am the mother Earth,  
Filled with water, air and life  
Humans, trees, birds, water animals and wildlife.  
I spin and turn all year round the orbit  
To give you all the seasons and climate.  

You people use my land, air, water and soil,  
But keep on filling it with litter and allowing it to spoil,  
I provide you air, food and home to live,  
With greedy lust, you destroy the nature I give  
And plot your own nature's death.  

The air you fill with smoke, makes my breath choke,  
You make my heart fill with strife  
But cutting down the green forests,  
And pushing up the greenhouse gases.  
What was once a clean sea and pure soil  
Is now all barren, clodded with waste and oil.  

How long can I stand the trouble,  
I start to scream and tremble  
I give you all I have, unto my end  
But it's now all in your hands to defend and mend.  

The earth—our mother earth, is the only planet which has life. Anju opened her eyes and remembered that she had arrived with her grandpa at his estate the previous night. She heard the chirping of birds and was overjoyed. She ran out of her bed to see the birds. But oh! she felt disgusted. As she looked out of the window of her room, she could see a heap of garbage outside. She could smell the foul smell too. She felt really bad.  

She rushed to talk to her grandpa. Grandpa had just returned from his morning walk. The estate manager was by his side. Grandpa was instructing him to dispose off the garbage.  

Anju wished her grandpa, “Good morning, grandpa.” “Good morning, my girl. But the foul smell of this garbage turns into a bad one. I have been trying to find out ways for proper garbage disposal. But all in vain.”  

“Grandpa, we just need a cleaner surrounding to live peacefully and happily. Even our school contributes to make our campus cleaner as part of ‘Swachh Bharat Abhiyan’.”  

“Yes, my child!” smiled grandpa. “This is India’s biggest ever cleanliness drive to make India clean!  

The only solution to this problem is 3R’s – reduce, reuse, recycle.  

**Reuse**  

It refers to using items more than once.  
- Reuse plastic bags, cloth bags, etc.  
- Reuse water bottles.  
- Reuse papers for making covers.  

**Reduce**  

We should reduce the waste generated.  
- Replace plastic shopping bags with reusable shopping bags.  
- Refuse to purchase products that use excessive packaging materials.  
- Reduce the waste generated by using steel utensils instead of disposable ones.  

**Recycle**  

We should use recycled goods.  
- Buy products made from recycled materials or with recyclable or reusable packaging.  
- Avoid packaging made of more than one material that cannot be separated or put in a waste recycling bin.  

If we follow the 3R's appropriately, we can surely save our earth and make it a better place to live.  

**Moral** : There is no other planet to live, so save the earth.  

## Overview  

What we take from the earth, we must give it back. This is the nature's law. Thinking differently, we all can change our habits. These small, worthwhile changes can create a great difference to save our earth. By being a little conscious, we can make a real difference for a better tomorrow.
