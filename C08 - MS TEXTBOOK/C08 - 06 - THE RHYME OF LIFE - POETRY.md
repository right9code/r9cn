# The Rhyme of Life  

## Let's Begin  

The rhyme of life  
Is the grace and care  
To brighten days  
With kindness shared.  

 So many times
 I've been in trouble
 Lost on a journey
 A day of struggle.  
 
Then, to find there  
Comes a stranger  
Helping, fixing,  
Calming danger.  

Changing a tyre  
Giving a gift  
'Please, no payment  
A simple gift.'  

Each human life  
Can touch another  
The difference made  
Will last forever.  

This rhyme of life  
I give to you  
In certain hope  
You'll find it true.  

**Moral :** One good turn deserves another.  

## Overview  

Kindness always pays. Every act of goodness or kindness you do today will return to you sooner or later in a rewarding way. So one should invest in good deeds. Remember, if you do so, God will fill your life's pockets with His blessings.
