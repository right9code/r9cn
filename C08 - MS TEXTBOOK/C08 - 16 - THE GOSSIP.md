# The Gossip  

## Let's Begin  

Once upon a time, there was a priest. One day, he was yawning and a small feather of a bird flew into his mouth. It accidentally got stuck in his throat. The priest tried to spit it out and after much trying it came out. The priest was surprised to see the feather as it was an egret—rare bird's feather. He wondered how it happened to be in that region.  

He reached his home and told his wife how an egret's feather had stuck in his throat. His wife was puzzled. The priest told her, 'Don't tell anyone about this as people will take it as a bad omen.' His wife agreed. But very soon, the desire to bring out the secret started troubling the lady. She was not able to sleep and said to herself, 'I have to tell about it to someone otherwise my stomach will burst.'  

So, the next day she called out her neighbour's wife and told her, 'I have a secret but I can't tell it to anyone. If you promise to keep the secret, I'll tell it to you.' The  

neighbour's wife agreed to keep the secret. The priest's wife told her all about the feather and also told her that, she was worried that it could be a bad omen. The neighbour's wife calmed her and went back. She too was thrilled about the story and now she was curious to tell about it to someone.  

As soon as the neighbour's wife met her friend, she told her about the priest and the feather. She also said, 'Please let it be a secret. Don't tell it to anyone.'  

The story sounded like a whole bunch of egret feathers had come out of the priest's mouth. Now, this friend agreed to keep it secret and replied, 'It's unbelievable... especially when we know that the priest is a pure vegetarian.'  

As soon as she reached home, she went to her the neighbour and told her that a whole egret  

came out of the priest's mouth. Soon the story spread throughout the village. It had changed from one feather to hundreds of feathers to a whole egret and a number of different birds of different sizes.  

People gathered at the priest's house to see this miracle. Now, the priest made the rumours clear, 'No bird came out of my mouth. It was just a small feather.'  

The people laughed that how the gossip had spread and changed the whole thing.  

**Moral :** Gossip can create huge problems. So, don't gossip.  

## Overview  

Gossips can be described as a casual or unconstrained conversation or report about other people, typically involving details which are not actually true. Gossip can create big problems. These can damage the reputation of people and defame them. It is a sheer wastage of time.
