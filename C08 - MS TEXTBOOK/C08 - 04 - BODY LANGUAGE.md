# Body Language  

## Let's Begin  

Mrs Arora picked up the phone and said, "Hello! Good morning! Principal speaking!" The voice at the other end sounded a bit disturbed, "Good morning, Ma'am. I am the father of Ramit of class VIII. I want to talk about something serious. Lately, he has developed some mannerisms which I feel are not good. I know he respects you a lot and would definitely follow your advice. So, could you guide and correct him?"  

Mrs Arora said, "Don't worry, Mr Khanna. I shall see that your problem is solved. Good day and take care."  

She immediately started on the action after the call dropped. She went to Ramit's class and sat at the back, observing the class. Her eyes were at Ramit and his friends consistently. After a few minutes, she walked out of the class. She understood and  

recognised the problem. Ramit and his friends had started imitating a famous TV serial character and were not attentive in the class. Mrs Arora decided to speak to her students about the importance of correct body language.  

Let's read what she explained to her students.  

Body language is the process of communicating non-verbally through body movements and gestures. This kind of language is considered the most important aspect of communication as it sends true signals about our feelings. As we perfect our own body language, we should at the same time, learn to interpret the body language of people around us.  

Basically, our body language is controlled by our subconscious mind and is not always in accordance with what we are saying.  

Hence, it is important to practise body language so that correct movements match our words. Thus, make sure that our thoughts and opinions are conveyed properly through our body as well as words.  

**Some basic tips for correct body language**  

- **Posture**  

When we are sitting or standing, our back should be perfectly straight but not stiff. Let your shoulders relax. This will convey the idea that you are comfortable.  
 
 - **Take up space**  

While sitting or standing, keep your legs a bit apart. This brings an idea that you are at ease.  

- **Lean**  

Lean slightly when someone is talking to you. This will signify that you are an active listener.  

- **Arms**  

Keep your arms comfortably to your side or bring your hands together in your lap to show others that you are open to the communication. Crossing your arms is a clue that you are turned off by what is going on around you.  

- **Handshake**  

The handshake is an important non-verbal communication. It can set the mood for the entire conversation. A firm handshake is a sign of affirmity, credibility and positivity whereas a weak handshake will appear fragile to you.  

- **Eye Contact**  

Proper eye contact during conversation shows your interest in the topic. Do stare at them but remember to look away occasionally.  

- **Positive movement**  

Simple actions like nodding your head or smiling gives affirmation to your conversation.  

**Like we have learnt about these positive movements, there are certain body postures that give a negative impression. Read about them and try to keep them away.**  

- **Looking at the ground**  

When someone is talking and the other person keeps looking at the ground, it tells that you are either shy or disinterested.  

- **Checking the time**  

Checking the time signals that you don't want to be there, instead you have more important tasks to do.  

- **Tapping**  

Tapping your fingers, feet or any object like a pen shows your impatience.  

- **On the edge**  

Sitting on the edge of the chair communicates your discomfort, both mentally as well as physically.  

- **Fake smile**  

People get to know when you are faking your smile. So do not even bother trying. If you need to smile, see that it comes from within.  

**Moral :** Accurate body language is essential for conveying your thoughts properly.  

## Overview  

Body language is more powerful than words. It expresses thoughts and intentions through physical behaviour such as facial expressions, body postures, touch and use of space. Your ability to understand and interpret body language can help you to pick up on unspoken issues or negative feeling in others.
