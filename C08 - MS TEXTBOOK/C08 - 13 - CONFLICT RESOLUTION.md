# Conflict Resolution  

## Let's Begin  

Once there was a father who left 17 camels as an asset for his three sons. When the father passed away, his sons opened up the will.  

The will of the father stated that the eldest son should get half of 17 camels while the middle son should be given one-third and the youngest son should be given one-ninth of the 17 camels.  

As it is not possible to divide 17 into half or 17 by 3 or 17 by 9, the three sons started quarrelling with one another. How could they divide their father's inheritance? So, the three sons decided to go to a wise man. The wise man listened patiently about the whole matter of dividing 17 camels.  

Then he gave a thought for a few minutes. He explained the division to the three sons.  

He said, "Let me add one of my camel to your camels. So you have 18 camels in total now."  

Now, he started reading the deceased father's will. He said, "The eldest son should get half share. So, half of 18 is 9." So, he gave 9 camels to the eldest son.  

Then he said, "One third of 18 is equal to 6." So, he gave the middle son 6 camels.  

Then he said, "One-ninth of 18 is 2. So, the youngest son will get 2 camels."  

The total was 9 plus 6 plus 2 which is 17 and one camel was left which the wise man took away and resolved the conflict easily.  

**Moral :** There is a solution of every problem if one know how to solve it, in a proper way.  

## Overview  

Whenever, there are problems, there are solutions. If we think that there is no solution, we won't be able to reach any!

## Let's Act  

**A. Learn conflict management tips.**  

| #   | **Manage conflicts effectively**                                                    |  
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |  
| 1   | Listen to what is said and respect the feelings of others.                          |  
| 2   | Know when to let something go. Conflicts cannot survive without your participation. |  
| 3   | Make conflict resolution your priority. It's not necessary to win the situation.    |  
| 4   | Decide whether the issue is really worthy of your time or attention.                |  
| 5   | Focus on the present. Never hold grudges because of the past.                       |  

**B. Make this conflict resolution wheel and follow when there is a conflict. This way you will not only learn it but also stay calm.**  

**C. You must have heard about conflict resolution from your adults. When it comes to conflict resolution, where do you stand, what do you do to be a conflict solver? Take this quick-self test and find out. Respond 'yes' or 'no' for each statement. If you get 5 or more yes, you are a good conflict solver:**  

☐ I listen to what the other person has to say.  
☐ I'm willing to compromise.  
☐ I try to calm down before I react.  
☐ I avoid unnecessary shouting.  
☐ I speak the truth, even if it is rude.  
☐ I speak the truth, but politely.  
☐ I try to put myself in the other person's place.  
☐ I look for ways to solve the problem rather than just win the argument.  
☐ I do my best to avoid physical fighting.  
☐ I try to see how I'm responsible instead of just blaming the other person.
