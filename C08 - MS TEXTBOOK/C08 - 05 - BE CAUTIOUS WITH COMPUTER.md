# Be Cautious with Computer  

## Let's Begin  

Sofia was busy doing her homework in her room when her father called her, 'Sofia, come here.'  

He was in the living room. Sofia went to the room and saw her father's angry face. Rather it was a horrified, upset face. Sofia got scared as she knew something was wrong. She saw another man, nearly of her father's age, sitting on the sofa.  

'Sit down Sofia,' said father. 'This man here is to tell a disturbing thing about you.' Sofia sat down, but she was terrified of what had happened. She had never seen the man before.  

'Do you know me, Sofia?' asked the man.  

'No, never,' said Sofia.  

'No? But I am your online friend, Joe,' said the man.  

Sofia was shocked. 'That's impossible. Joe is a girl of my age. And she lives in Toronto,' said Sofia.  

'Ha!Ha!Ha!' smiled the man. 'I know. I told you all that but it wasn't true,' said the man. Sofia was shocked.  

'You see, Sofia, there are many fake accounts online and the account holders pretend to be someone else. They do it to cheat others and lead them astray. But I am a police officer. I came here to tell you how foolish you have been. You gave me so many details about yourself and so I am here. I had a friend whose daughter was of your age. She, fell into a trap of a fake account holder and got herself into a great danger.  

Hearing so, Sofia was disturbed. The man continued, 'You too, could have got yourself into a big trouble if you give out information online to strangers. They can turn out to be wickeds.'  

Sofia burst into cries and ran to hug her father. The police officer said, 'Never share your personal details online. You were lucky that it was me.'  

'Sir, I will never do such a foolish thing again,' said Sofia. The man walked out of their house. She was thankful to the man. He had saved her from getting trapped into a dangerous situation.  

We all should be very cautious while working with computers. Here are some set of rules or guidance for appropriate behaviour that we should follow while using the Internet. These set of rules are called **Netiquettes**.  

**Netiquettes:**  

1. Everything you post online is public. When you post things online, you are creating cyber fingerprint. It's irrelevant even if you delete it. It can be traced.  
2. Mind your language and be kind. You may be looking at just a computer screen but there is another person on the receiving end.  
3. Never post pictures of yourself or others unless you don't mind them being shared with everyone. Be very selective about the content you upload.  
4. Don't give personal information online. Never share your full name, address, cell number, password, credit card number or any other confidential information online.  
5. Do not use ALL CAPS when you are e-mailing or posting something. All CAPS is a form of internet shouting.  
6. Don't post things when you are angry. Take some time to clear your head before you deal with the situation. This will help you not to say something which you might regret later.  
7. Use different usernames or passwords for safety.  
8. Set up an impressive e-mail address. You will use your e-mail address in future for college applications, job applications, resume and scholarship opportunities.  
9. Never get into chatrooms and reveal confidential things about you.  
10. Be honest and clear. Do not copy or claim credit for someone else's work.  

**Moral :** Be smart online. Be cautious.  

## Overview  

The internet is one of the best resources available to us. Unfortunately, it's also extremely dangerous if you aren't aware of who and what lurks behind the scenes. Everyone should know how to be safe when surfing web. Ensure that no information is shared on internet which can harm you.
