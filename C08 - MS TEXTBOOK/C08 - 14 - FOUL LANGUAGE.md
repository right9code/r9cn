# Foul Language  

## Let's Begin  

Rick was a nice young boy who had a very bad habit of getting angry at every little thing. He would easily get angry with anyone and would even use foul language sometimes. His parents and teachers often lectured him to control his anger and improve his behaviour.  

Rick had a soft corner for paintings. So on his 9th birthday his mom gifted him a canvas and a palette of colours.  

**Rick** : What's this for?  

**Mom** : It's to help you express your anger. Whatever you feel angry about, you can paint it on the canvas to get rid of that frustration. Try it.  

Rick painted a building falling down.  

It's what I want to do to my school. Rick said I hate everyone there. The kids keep bullying and criticising me. I feel so angry with them.  

Oh! Rick! Okay. Do it again.  

**Mom** : Paint whatever you feel inside and whatever you think what to do.  

'Rick took another sheet and started painting. From that day, whatever made Rick angry had to be painted on a sheet. By the weekend Rick had painted several disturbing paintings. His mom went to his room and found some of the paintings on the wall. She collected some and took Rick to the garden, with his paintings.'  

**Mom** : Tell me Rick, what are these paintings about?  

**Rick** : It's about those kids that always act so smart, they flash their expensive clothes and phones and make me look like a loser. It's about the teachers who keep saying to me that I don't perform well enough. It's about dad who always keeps me reminding of how well he used to do when he was of my age.  

**Mom** : Hmm, Now think and tell me what is the one thing common in all of them.  

**Rick** : I am angry at all of them.  

**Mom** : Exactly! You reacted to each of those persons who said something bad about you. Your anger was in their power, not in yours.  

**Rick** : You're right. Infact, while painting this, I threw the colours on the paintings, imagining that I was throwing something at them. I never tried to understand them and got angry all along without any reason.  

**Mom** : Son, each bad word that you said to them left a hole in their heart. Those kids did well for themselves not to put you down. Your teachers tell you to improve because they know your potential and your dad compares himself with you only to inspire you, to tell you that if he was able to do it, so can you.  

**Rick** : You're right mom, but what should I do when I get angry. It's always like anger overpowers me and I am never able to think properly.  

**Mom** : Controlling anger and foul language can be simple only if you are committed to control it. Whenever you get angry, take a deep breath and count up to 20 before you react. Think happy thoughts and divert yourself from that situation. Slowly you will be able to control your anger and language.  

**Rick** : Thanks Mom! I will try to do this.  

**Moral :** Everybody should be aware of anger and using foul language. They are enemies.  

## Overview  

Anger sucks up our energy and empties our brain. Foul language makes only enemies. We have to fight with them.

Talk to the student not to become rude towards anybody, parents or friends. Teach them to some new way to calm down when they get angry. Anger harms mind, body and soul. So ask them to invent some new way to overcome anger.
