# Decision Making  

## Let's Begin  

A kid went into a candy store with his dad and was bedazzled by the treats that he could choose. 'What should I choose? What should I choose?'— he asked himself. 'Come on son, we don't have all day,' his dad said. 'These are my favourites,' 'no wait', 'these are my favourites', he walked along the aisles, picking up bags and putting them back, he just couldn't make up his mind.  

'Quick son, make up your mind, we have to go',— his impatient dad said frantically. The boy ran around the store; his eyes moving from one shelf to another but all of the options looked so good that he couldn't make a decision. Eventually, the dad had grabbed his son by the hand and they walked out of the store emptyhanded. The young boy had tears in his eyes. He wanted them all but ended up with nothing.  

because he couldn't choose just one. We are all that boy and, the world is that candy store. We have endless number of options available to us but sometimes we spend minute after minute, hour after hour trying to find the absolutely perfect choice. We might spend days or even weeks trying to figure out which choice is best. Sometimes we worry about making the wrong choice. What! If we regret the direction that we take and it's too late to go back, the bigger danger is that we make no decision at all and end up going nowhere. This world is all about evolving, growing, succeeding failing and learning from those choices that weren't just right. Don't spend your entire life overthinking about the perfect choice because most of the time one doesn't exist.  

**Moral :** Good decision making is the result of years of experience, learning from one's choices and that are both good and bad.  

## Overview  

Success in life depends on, decision you make at a time when it requires. After passing of time, decision making ability loses its importance just like a heavy shower after the drying of crops. So, don't spend entire life overthinking and waiting for a perfect choice.
