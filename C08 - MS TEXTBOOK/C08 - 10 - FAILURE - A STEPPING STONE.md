# Failure — A Stepping Stone  

## Let's Begin  

Failure is a common word. Many a times, we have to face failures in our lives.  

But what does this word actually mean? Failure is actually the negative state of mind in which one sees no good and no progress. It is exactly the opposite of positive thinking and motivation.  

When we think about failures, two contradictory thoughts come in our mind. One is that we forget about our achievements and keep on pondering over our failures. The other one is that we forget our failures and keep on pondering over our achievements. The first thought is a common  

one because it is the human tendency to think about failures. It can lead  

to depression even. It is a negative approach to life. But the second thought asks us to think positively in spite of our failures. It helps us to receive energy and think about the future enthusiastically. It's called a positive outlook towards life. It guides us to learn from our failures, to do better and to succeed the next time.  

Generally, people are depressed by their failures. They are upset and this makes their lives painful and burdensome. They are not able to think positively and forget all about their achievements. But we should develop a positive outlook about failures. If you think positively, it will surely help you to reach success one day.  

One should analyze his/her failure and its reasons constructively and work on them. This will help him/her to repeat such acts and once you know the cause of your failure, you can easily rectify it.  

In order to change our life, our failures need to be transformed into success. If we make a proper analysis, we will come to know that these failures can actually be very helpful. These are actually the stepping stones in life. Failures encourages us to work hard, so that we can achieve our goals and become successful in life. One who learns from his failures he/she learns to begin positivity.  

No man is perfect. But we all are heading towards perfection. So, the failures are only breakers in life, but not a full stop. For human beings, it is natural to make mistakes and learn from them.  

All the discoverers and inventors did not invent something in one go. They had to try many times and only then, they were able to create something great. Great writers didn't become famous with their first book, instead they spent many years of their life to get perfection and fame.  

Everything starts from a simple idea and finally becomes great after a long chain of efforts and hard work.  

However, in life, many times, we meet failures. What we should do then-  

**1. Accept it**  

What's done is done? You can only accept it now. Don't think about it again and again. There is no point worrying about something that cannot be changed. Instead, you should use your energy to affect the future by acting consciously.  

**2. Learn from it**  

The best thing is to learn a lesson from what has happened. This will help you to work better the next time.  

**3. Analyze it**  

Talk about the situation with your family or friends whom you trust. They will surely give you support and guidance. You should also analyze the reason of your failure yourself.  

**4. Be grateful to it**  

Take your failure as a gift. Later it will make you feel better.  

**5. Prepare yourself for the next attempt**  

Use the information from your analysis and try not to repeat any of the things that led to failure.  

**6. Don't lose enthusiasm**  

You should try to become more enthusiastic this time.  

**7. Work harder**  

You should always work harder to achieve your goal.  

**8. Set your mind on your goal**  

Think positive, think success, and always be ready for hard work.  

**9. Have faith in God**  

Do it again. Do it now. Do it better.  

Don't doubt your ability to achieve your goal. Keep your head up. Just trust God and pray to Him.  

**Moral :** Failures are the stepping stones for success  

## Overview  

Failure makes us sad. It's just the opposite of positive thinking and motivation. Every failure should be assessed well so that it can be overcome. Remember, to err is human. So don't worry about the mistakes. Just be positive, work hard and believe in almighty.
