# Sportsmanship  

## Let's Begin  

When the battle lines are drawn  
And you have valiantly stood your ground  
But once it finally dawns  
That you have lost, then get off your high horse.  

Step back with grace and accept defeat  
It may be taken as a sign of weakness  
But you know, it requires strength to gracefully retreat  
And to congratulate your adversary and shake hands.  

You are enveloped with a sense of peace  
In life, love, sports or war, you win some  
And lose some, that's the way it is.  
If everyone tries to have the last word, there will be anarchy.  

**Moral :** Win or lose, showing good sportsmanship can go a long way.  

## Overview  

Sports bring out our competitive nature, but winning isn't everything. Play at your best. An act of good sportsmanship is remembered for a lifetime. A good sportsman respects both his teammates and his opponents equally.

## Let's Act  

**A. To be a good sports player develop the following qualities:**  

1. Play Fair  
Never cheat or play dirty to win. Remember it's just a game. If you cheat, you may be victorious for a short term. But it will forever hurt your conscience.  

2. Be a team player  
Do your best to get the whole team involved.  
Don't play for yourself, play for the team.  

3. Lose gracefully  
In any sports, there will be winners and losers.  
When you lose don't sulk. On the other hand,  
give the other team congratulatory handshake.  

4. Win with class  
If you win, don't gloat or put down the other team after your win. Let your game performance speak for itself.  

5. Respect the rulings of officials  
Remember to respect the rulings of referees and umpires. They may be wrong so make your appeal calmly. If by any chance they stand by their ruling, accept it and move on. They are also human beings and can make errors sometimes.
