# Healthy Diet  

## Let's Begin  

Healthy food is very important in my day,  
My body needs nutrition to run and play.  
Proteins keep my hair shiny and my muscles strong,  
Carbohydrates give me energy all day long.  
Fruits and vegetables have the vitamins I need,  
Fats are good for my skin and help my brain succeed.  
Dairy helps to keep my teeth and bones tough,  
Milk, cheese and yogurt have all the right stuff.  
A healthy, balanced diet has all the food groups in it,  
Combined with exercise, I'll be healthy and fit.  

**Moral :** A balanced diet and exercise is the wonderful tonic of our life.  

## Overview  

We should observe healthy eating routine to enhance our physique and to keep us away from diseases. Exercises keep us agile the whole day.
