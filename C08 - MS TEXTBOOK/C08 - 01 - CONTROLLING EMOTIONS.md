# Controlling Emotions  

## Let's Begin  

Mona was a young girl. She was growing, she experienced chaotic changes in her emotions every now and then. She was not willing to go out anywhere or with anybody.  

One day Mona shouted, "I am in no mood, mom. Please don't disturb me." Her mom came to her and held her close. "What happened my dear child? Why are you so frustrated? Aunt Rose wants to spend a weekend with you," said her mom.  

There was a frown on Mona's face, "Oh mom, it's so boring at Aunt Rose's house. I have no friends to play with. Moreover, I am in no mood to go anywhere today!"  

She banged her feet and went into her room and banged the door shut. The poor mother sighed and worriedly sat on the chair.  

• **Find a healthy distraction and be positive**  

Find some creative things that interest you so that you may be distracted from your bad mood. Hobbies are a perfect way to change your mood.  

• **Catch your breath**  

Count to 10 or do something that lets you settle down for a few moments.  

Mona followed these simple steps. After a few days, she felt better and was able to cope up with her mood swings very soon.  

**Moral :** Try to change your bad mood into good by following some simple means.  

## Overview  

Mood swing is a normal part of growth or adolescence and can be a healthy emotional response to outside stressors. Being able to choose the mood that is best suited to situation is one of the skills of emotional intelligence. Choosing the right mindset can help you control whatever situation you are facing.
