# CLASS VII (ADJECTIVES)

**A. Fill in the blanks with the correct alternative.**  
1. Bring me `____` chair. (**these/that**)  
2. Mr Roy is a man of `____` words. (**few/a few**)  
3. It was a `____` opportunity. (**terrific/terrifying**)  
4. The plan was an `____` failure. (**total/absolute**)  
5. He is `____` to me by a few years. (**senior/superior**)  

**C. Pick out the participial adjectives from these sentences. Not all sentences will have them.**  
1. The injured students got off the bus.  
2. Ashwini played the winning stroke.  
3. The tiger was seriously wounded.  
4. She is an experienced teacher.  
5. The frightened rabbits ran away.
