**A. Fill in the blanks with suitable personal or possessive pronouns.**  

1. My father is furious with `**_____ME_______**`. I have lost a favourite book of `**_____HIS_______**`.  
2. 'Who did this?' Mother asked.  
    'It was `**______ME/I______**`. But the idea was `______HIS/HERS______`,' replied Akash.
3. Mr Rathore is a nominee for the elections. Between you and `_____ME_______`, `_____HE_______` is a truly deserving candidate.  


**C. 
1. I did not buy **either** of the two pens. (Replaced "neither" – Distributive Pronoun)  
2. I filled up the forms **myself**. (Replaced "herself" – Reflexive / Emphasising Pronoun)  
3. One must be careful about what **one** says. (Replaced "he" – Indefinite Pronoun)  
4. 'You will only do well if you help **each other / one another**,' the coach told the team. (Replaced "one" – Reciprocal Pronoun)  
5. Jaya **herself** invited her friends for the party. (Replaced "himself" – Emphasising Pronoun)
