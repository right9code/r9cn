### CLASS VIII (ENGLISH GRAMMAR)  
**Topic: Adjectives**  
**Maximum Marks: 10**  

**A. Underline the adjective in each sentence and state its kind. (3 × 1 = 3 Marks)**  

1. Which fruit is your favourite?  
2. I have bought enough supplies to last me a week.  
3. The naughty cat was playing happily with the owlet.  

**B. Identify the adjective and the noun it describes in the following sentences. (3 × 1 = 3 Marks)**  

1. The enormous mansion had four minarets.  
2. Twinkling bulbs of many hues added to the charm.  
3. His loyal retainers rushed to greet him.  

**C. Rewrite these sentences using the adjective forms of the bold words without altering the meaning. (4 × 1 = 4 Marks)**  

1. I was surprised by his **foolishness**.  
2. He behaved like a **child**.  
3. He spoke with **bluntness**.  
4. You should maintain **silence** in the library.

### Answer Key (Fully Solved)  

**A. Underline the adjective in each sentence and state its kind.**  

1. **Which** fruit is your favourite?  
   **Kind:** Interrogative Adjective *(Note: 'your' is also correct as a Possessive Adjective)*  
2. I have bought **enough** supplies to last me a week.  
   **Kind:** Adjective of Quantity  
3. The **naughty** cat was playing happily with the owlet.  
   **Kind:** Adjective of Quality  

**B. Identify the adjective and the noun it describes in the following sentences.**  

1. Adjective: **enormous** -> Noun: **mansion**  
   *(Also correct: Adjective: four -> Noun: minarets)*  
2. Adjective: **Twinkling** -> Noun: **bulbs**  
   *(Also correct: Adjective: many -> Noun: hues)*  
3. Adjective: **loyal** -> Noun: **retainers**  
   *(Also correct: Adjective: His -> Noun: retainers)*  

**C. Rewrite these sentences using the adjective forms of the bold words without altering the meaning.**  

1. His **foolish** behaviour surprised me.  
2. He exhibited **childish** behaviour. *(or: His behaviour was childish.)*  
3. He spoke in a **blunt** manner. *(or: His speech was blunt.)*  
4. You should remain **silent** in the library.
