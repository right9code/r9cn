5. Identify the tenses:

	ii. I am playing a game.
	iii. `Question word + do/does + subject + first form of verb + ...?`  



---





































# ANSWERS:
### Helping Verbs in Present Tenses
* **i. Simple Present:** Do / Does
* **ii. Present Continuous:** Is / Am / Are
* **iii. Present Perfect:** Has / Have
* **iv. Present Perfect Continuous:** Has been / Have been

---

### Tense Rules & Features

* **Which present tenses use -ing form of verb?**
  * Present Continuous
  * Present Perfect Continuous

* **Which present tense uses 3rd form/past participle of verb?**
  * Present Perfect

* **Which present tense adds s/es with verb in SS(III)?**
  * Simple Present (Affirmative)

---

### Answer Key: Identify the Tenses

i. Singular subject + has + been + first form of verb + ing + ... + for/since +...
   -> **Present Perfect Continuous Tense**

ii. I am playing a game.
   -> **Present Continuous Tense**

iii. Question word + do/does + subject + first form of verb + ...?
   -> **Simple Present Tense (Interrogative)**
