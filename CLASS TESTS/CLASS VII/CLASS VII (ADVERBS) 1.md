### CLASS VII (ENGLISH GRAMMAR)  
**Topic: Adverbs**  
**Maximum Marks: 10**  

**A. Fill in the blanks with the kind of adverb mentioned in the brackets. (4 × 1 = 4 Marks)**  

1. She arrived `____` for the class. *(adverb of time)*  
2. We walked `____` in the direction of the school. *(adverb of manner)*  
3. The grains were scattered `____`. *(adverb of place)*  
4. Breakfast is `____` ready. *(adverb of degree)*  

**B. State whether the highlighted words are adverbs or adjectives. (3 × 1 = 3 Marks)**  

1. The teacher dictated the notes in a **loud** voice.  
2. He visits his mother **daily**.  
3. Work **hard** if you wish to do well.  

**C. Rewrite the sentences by inserting the adverbs given in the brackets in their correct positions. (3 × 1 = 3 Marks)**  

1. Sam agreed to the plan. **(readily)**  
2. I asked for a glass of milk. **(only)**  
3. He was brave to challenge the authorities. **(enough)**

### Answer Key (Fully Solved)  

**A. Fill in the blanks with the kind of adverb mentioned in the brackets.**  

1. She arrived **late** for the class.  
2. We walked **briskly** in the direction of the school.  
3. The grains were scattered **everywhere**.  
4. Breakfast is **almost** ready.  

**B. State whether the highlighted words are adverbs or adjectives.**  

1. The teacher dictated the notes in a **loud** voice. – **Adjective**  
2. He visits his mother **daily**. – **Adverb**  
3. Work **hard** if you wish to do well. – **Adverb**  

**C. Rewrite the sentences by inserting the adverbs given in the brackets in their correct positions.**  

1. Sam **readily** agreed to the plan.  
2. I **only** asked for a glass of milk.  
3. He was brave **enough** to challenge the authorities.
