### Answer Key (Fully Solved)  

---

**Q1. Identify and write the HELPING VERBS (Auxiliary Verbs) used in the following tenses:**  
*Fill in the blanks.*  

**i.** Simple Past: ` did ` *(used in negative and interrogative sentences)*  
**ii.** Past Continuous: ` was / were `  
**iii.** Past Perfect: ` had `  
**iv.** Past Perfect Continuous: ` had been `  


**Q2. Which Past tense(s) use the "-ing" (Present Participle) form of the verb?**  
**Ans:** ` Past Continuous and Past Perfect Continuous `  


**Q3. Which Past tense uses the 3rd form (Past Participle) of the verb?**  
**Ans:** ` Past Perfect `  


**Q4. Which Past tense strictly uses the 2nd form of the verb?**  
**Ans:** ` Simple Past ` *(in affirmative/positive sentences)*  


**Q5. Identify the exact Past tense for the following sentence and structural formulas:**  

**i.** Singular subject + had + been + first form of verb + ing + ... + for/since + ...  
**Ans:** ` Past Perfect Continuous `  

**ii.** "I played a game."  
**Ans:** ` Simple Past `  

**iii.** Question word + did + subject + first form of verb + ...?  
**Ans:** ` Simple Past (Interrogative / Wh- Question) `
