**CLASS VIII (ENGLISH GRAMMAR)**

**Topic: Future Tenses — Answer Key**

---

### Q1. Identify and write the HELPING VERBS (Auxiliary Verbs) used in the following tenses:

i. Simple Future: **will / shall**

ii. Future Continuous: **will be / shall be**

iii. Future Perfect: **will have / shall have**

iv. Future Perfect Continuous: **will have been / shall have been**

---

### Q2. Which Future tense(s) use the "-ing" (Present Participle) form of the verb?

Ans: **Future Continuous Tense** and **Future Perfect Continuous Tense**

---

### Q3. Which Future tense uses the 3rd form (Past Participle) of the verb?

Ans: **Future Perfect Tense**

*(Note: The 3rd form of the verb is standardly called the Past Participle.)*

---

### Q4. What helping verb is used with I/We traditionally?

Ans: **shall**

---

### Q5. Identify the exact Future tense for the following sentence and structural formulas:

i. Singular subject + will + have + been + first form of verb + ing + ... + for/since + ...

Ans: **Future Perfect Continuous Tense**

ii. "I will play a game."

Ans: **Simple Future Tense**

iii. Question word + will + subject + be ...?

Ans: **Future Continuous Tense** *(Interrogative structure)*
