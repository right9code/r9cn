## Chapter Details

### Main 01 — [[C10 - M - 01 - A LETTER TO GOD|A Letter to God]]

| Character | Who they are |
|-----------|--------------|
| Lencho | A poor, hardworking farmer whose crops are destroyed by hail; writes a letter to God asking for 100 pesos and has unshakable faith |
| Lencho's wife | preparing supper when the hailstorm begins; replies "God willing" when Lencho says they will not go hungry |
| Lencho's sons | working in the field alongside their father, replying "Amen" to his prayers |
| Postman | the postmaster's co-worker who finds the letter addressed to God and laughs heartily about it |
| Postmaster | a fat, amiable man who arranges for the postal employees to send money and signs the reply letter "God" to protect Lencho's faith |
| Post office employees | colleagues of the postmaster who each contribute part of the money; Lencho later calls them "a bunch of crooks" for keeping part of the sum |
