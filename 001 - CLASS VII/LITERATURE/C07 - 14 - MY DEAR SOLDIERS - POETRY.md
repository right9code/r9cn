# MY DEAR SOLDIERS  

Oh! Defenders of borders  
You are great sons of my land  
When we are all asleep  
You still hold on to your deed  
Windy season or snowy days  
Or scorching sun's sweltering rays  
You are there guarding all the time awake  
Treading the lonely expanses as yogis  
Climbing the heights or striding the valleys  
Defending the deserts or guarding the marshes  
Surveillance in seas and by securing the air  
Prime of your youth given to the nation!!  
Wind chimes of my land vibrate your feat  
We pray for you brave men!!  
May the Lord bless you all!!  

**A.P.J. ABDUL KALAM**

# Answers – My Dear Soldiers  

## Let us discuss  

**I. Complete the summary with an exact word from the poem.**  

The poem is a tribute to the soldiers of the 1. **land** who defend the 2. **borders** of their country. It highlights their untiring commitment and sacrifices as they guard the nation. The soldiers remain vigilant and dedicated, whether it's windy, snowy, or 3. **scorching** hot. They walk on 4. **lonely** stretches of land like the yogis. Their duty takes them to different terrains, including mountains, valleys, 5. **deserts**, and marshes. The poet acknowledges that they have given a major part of their 6. **youth** to protect the nation. The poet says the wind chimes show their respect to the soldiers' 7. **feat**. The poet concludes with prayers for their well-being and blessings.  

**II. Select the correct option for the following questions.**  

1. The theme of the poem is **(i) patriotism and sacrifice**.  
2. The tone of the poem is **D. (i), (iii), (iv) and (v)** [admiring, respectful, grateful, prayerful].  

**III. Complete the following sentences by explaining why the poet has used the following poetic devices.**  

1. **Imagery** because it creates vivid mental pictures of soldiers guarding the nation in extreme weather, high mountains, deep valleys, and vast deserts.  
2. **Simile** because the soldiers are likened to yogis who live disciplined, selfless lives in quiet and lonely expanses.  
3. **Metaphor** because the poet uses wind chimes to represent nature celebrating and echoing the brave achievements of the soldiers.  
4. **Repetition** of the words 'you' and 'we' because it highlights the contrast between the soldiers who protect the borders and the citizens who sleep safely at home.  
5. **Alliteration** such as 'season or snowy' and 'scorching sun's sweltering' to create a rhythmic sound and emphasize the harsh weather conditions faced by soldiers.

**II. Answer the following questions.**  

1. **Who is the speaker of this poem? How can you say so?**  
The speaker of the poem is Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. His name is written at the end of the poem, and he expresses gratitude to soldiers on behalf of all citizens.  

2. **How is the theme of the poem conveyed through imagery? Support your answer with examples from the poem.**  
The theme of patriotism and sacrifice is conveyed through vivid imagery of difficult terrains and weather. Examples such as "snowy days", "scorching sun's sweltering rays", "climbing the heights", and "surveillance in seas" create clear mental pictures of the hard conditions soldiers endure.  

3. **How does the poet highlight the commitment of the soldiers? Provide examples from the poem to support your answer.**  
The poet highlights their commitment by showing that soldiers stay alert and guard the country day and night while citizens sleep. They defend mountains, valleys, deserts, marshes, seas, and air continuously.  

4. **What is the significance of the line 'Prime of your youth given to the nation!!' with reference to sacrifice and service?**  
This line signifies that soldiers dedicate the best and most energetic years of their lives to serving the nation, choosing national protection over personal comforts.  

5. **The poem ends with a prayer for the soldiers' well-being and blessings. Why is it important for each one of us to pray for the well-being of soldiers?**  
It is important to pray for soldiers because they risk their lives every day in dangerous environments to keep us safe. Praying for them shows our respect, gratitude, and moral support for their sacrifices.
