## Let us think and reflect  

**I. Read the extract given below and answer the questions that follow.**  

1. What does the word 'tribute' mean in this context?  
In this context, 'tribute' means a regular payment made by one ruler or state to a dominant power to show submission or secure protection.  

2. Complete the sentence with a suitable reason.  
*Rani Abbakka says, "We are a sovereign country" because her state has full power to govern itself without taking orders from foreign rulers.*

*The Portuguese envoy threatened Rani Abbakka.*  
True.

5. Choose the option that lists the qualities of Rani Abbakka, based on this extract.  
**C. (i), (iii), and (v)** [bold, capable, and determined]  

**II. Answer the following questions.**  

1. **Rani Abbakka spoke to her mother on her deathbed. How might these words have made her mother feel?**  
Rani Abbakka's promise to protect Ullal's freedom and never surrender to the Portuguese would have made her mother feel proud, peaceful, and confident that the kingdom was in capable hands.  

2. **What does the support of the people of Ullal and the soldiers for Rani Abbakka suggest about their mindset?**  
It suggests that the people and soldiers were deeply patriotic, loyal to their queen, and valued their freedom and dignity over living under foreign control.  

3. **How might the people of Ullal have felt when the mighty Portuguese were defeated?**  
The people of Ullal would have felt immense joy, pride, relief, and strong faith in Rani Abbakka's leadership when the Portuguese were defeated.  

4. **Why do you think Rani Abbakka's victory against the Portuguese would have inspired other rulers to go against a foreign power?**  
Her victory showed other rulers that even a small kingdom could defeat a powerful colonial force through courage, unity, and strong determination.  

## Let us write  

**Conversation between Pratap and Tarana on the role of women in building India's identity:**  

**Pratap** : Tarana, I have a lot of respect for women like Rani Abbakka, who fought fearlessly against foreign invaders to protect our motherland.  
**Tarana** : I believe in the capabilities of women. Did you know that women in modern India are leading major achievements in defense, space research, and science?  
**Pratap** : Yes, absolutely! Women officers are serving in all branches of the Armed Forces and guarding our borders with great courage.  
**Tarana** : True, and female scientists played a crucial role in India's Moon and Mars missions, making our nation proud globally.  
**Pratap** : Women are also excelling in sports, winning international medals and raising India's flag high at the Olympics.  
**Tarana** : In education, healthcare, and governance, women are leading positive changes in society every day.  
**Pratap** : Their dedication and hard work prove that women are equal partners in building a strong and progressive India.  
**Tarana** : Exactly, Pratap! By honoring their contributions, we inspire future generations to work for the nation's success.
