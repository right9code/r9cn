# Narrative Writing  

There are different ways of going about writing a story, and there are no set guidelines that can be followed to ensure a good short story. However, one of the first things to remember is that for your story to be a success, you have to hold the reader's attention.  

Here are some points to keep in mind when writing a short story.  
- Decide on a situation—this could be a problem that has to be resolved by the main character or a set of characters. This situation can depend on the title or theme that you are given. The plot is the most important aspect of a short story. Decide the main events and try to think of a twist at the end. Your story must have a beginning, a middle and a satisfactory ending. Everything could end unhappily for all the characters, but at least the reader must know what happened to the people in the story.  
- Choose a narrative point of view. You can write your story in the first person (as if you were one of the characters), or in the third person (as a detached narrator who presents the thoughts and observations of several characters). If you write in the first person, be consistent about the use of the pronoun 'I'.  
- Create a list of characters. Try to give them individual characteristics. Focus more on the main character.  
- Establish believable characters and settings, with vivid descriptions and dialogues, to create a story that your readers will care about. Remember that you have to work within a limited number of words; you cannot go on for pages, so you must use language effectively. Appeal to your readers' five senses to make your settings more real.  
- Never underestimate the power of dialogue in revealing the character of a person or the complexity of a situation, but in a short story, there is no place for long speeches. Every word you put into the mouth of your characters must contribute to revealing your theme.

# Answers – Narrative Writing  


**A.**  

1.  
It was a stormy night. The rain was pouring down heavily and the wind was so strong that many trees were uprooted. I was returning home after visiting Vivek when my bicycle chain suddenly snapped near the desolate stretch beside the old city lake. Darkness engulfed the road as streetlights flickered and died due to power failure.  

Standing under a leaking bus shelter, I heard a faint whimpering sound coming from a muddy ditch. Shining my phone flashlight, I discovered a tiny puppy trapped beneath a fallen tree branch, shivering in the freezing rain. Without hesitation, I waded through the mud, lifted the heavy branch with all my strength, and wrapped the frightened puppy in my raincoat. Carrying the little creature safely in my arms, I walked the remaining two miles home. The puppy survived and became my loyal companion, reminding me that even the stormiest nights can bring unexpected blessings.  

2.  
The sun was setting behind the snow-capped Himalayan peaks, painting the sky in brilliant shades of crimson and gold. We had spent five arduous hours trekking through dense pine forests to reach the scenic high-altitude meadow of Dayara Bugyal. Our legs were exhausted, but the panoramic beauty unfolding before our eyes erased every bit of fatigue.  

Our trekking guide pitched our tents beside a crystal-clear mountain stream, while we gathered around a small campfire to share hot tea and stories. As night descended, millions of bright stars glittered across the clear night sky, creating a breathtaking canopy above us. Sitting quietly under that vast cosmic expanse, surrounded by trusted friends and peaceful silence, I felt a deep sense of serenity and gratitude. I shall always cherish that memory.  

3.  
The water was cold, I took a deep breath and jumped into the rushing river. The sudden shock of icy mountain water surged through my body, sending a sharp shiver down my spine. But there was no time to panic; my young cousin had accidentally lost his footing on the slippery riverbank and was being carried downstream by the swift current.  

Kicking hard against the powerful swirl of the current, I swam vigorously toward the bright yellow jacket floating near the bend. Reaching him just before the river tumbled into a rocky rapid, I grabbed his collar securely with one arm and pulled his head above water. Using my remaining energy, I kicked toward a calm eddy along the left bank, where helpful bystanders pulled us both onto solid ground. Gasping for air on the grassy bank, I held my coughing cousin tightly, relieved that my decisive leap had saved his life.  

4.  
When Rohan lost his right arm in a tragic train accident at the age of twelve, everyone believed his passion for painting was permanently destroyed. His canvas easel sat abandoned in his room, covered in dust, while he struggled with deep sadness for months. However, watching a documentary about adaptive artists reignited a spark of determination within his heart.  

He began practicing patiently every single day, training his left hand to hold brushes, mix oil paints, and execute delicate strokes. Initial attempts resulted in smudges and frustrating failures, but Rohan refused to give up. After three years of relentless practice, he submitted his artwork to the National Youth Art Exhibition. To everyone's astonishment, his painting won the first prize for extraordinary technique and emotional depth. Standing proudly on stage to receive his trophy, Rohan proved to the world that where there is a will, there is always a way.  

**B.**  

1.  
**The Missing Briefcase**  
Inspector Sharma was standing near the central fountain of the crowded botanical garden when a frantic tourist approached him. The tourist explained that while taking photographs with his vintage camera, he had placed a black leather briefcase containing important property documents on a nearby bench. When he turned back moments later, the briefcase had vanished.  

Inspector Sharma asked the photographer to show him the pictures he had taken over the last ten minutes. Carefully reviewing the high-resolution images on the camera screen, the policeman noticed a man in a red jacket picking up the briefcase in the background of a floral landscape photo. Inspector Sharma quickly alerted the park exit gates with the visual description. Within fifteen minutes, security guards intercepted the suspect attempting to board a taxi. The briefcase was recovered intact, proving that the camera and the quick-thinking policeman were instrumental in solving the crime.  

2.  
**Light in the Darkness**  
The Roy family was preparing for dinner on a hot summer evening when an unexpected power cut plunged the entire apartment complex into total darkness. Sweltering heat quickly filled the rooms, making everyone irritable. Rohan and his elder sister began complaining bitterly, interrupting their mother's attempts to light emergency candles.  

Seeing the mood souring, their grandfather smiled and suggested a traditional story session on the open balcony. As gentle night breezes blew, he recounted fascinating tales from his youth, filled with humor, adventure, and wisdom. The children listened spellbound, forgetting their discomfort and putting aside their electronic gadgets. When the electricity was finally restored two hours later, no one wanted the magical evening to end. The sudden power cut had unexpectedly brought the family closer together.  

3.  
**Escaping the Shadow**  
Walking back home through the quiet alleyways of Old Town at dusk, Sameer noticed a tall figure in a dark trench coat keeping pace behind him. Every time Sameer accelerated his steps, the footsteps behind him quickened as well. Panic seized his mind as he realized he was being pursued through the dimly lit streets.  

Knowing the geography of his neighborhood well, Sameer pretended to walk toward a dead-end alley. Just as his pursuer turned the corner behind him, Sameer ducked swiftly behind a heavy wooden gate and slipped into the back courtyard of a busy bakery. Slipping out through the bakery's bright front entrance onto the main crowded boulevard, he flagged down a passing police patrol car and reported the suspicious tracker. Safe among the crowd, Sameer took a deep sigh of relief, thankful for his quick wits.  

4.  
**Opening New Doors**  
When Priya failed to clear the final interview for her dream medical college admission, she felt utterly heartbroken. For weeks she stayed indoors, mourning the closed door of her ambition and ignoring all words of comfort from family and friends. She could not stop brooding over what she had lost.  

Encouraged by her high school biology teacher, Priya reluctantly enrolled in a degree program in Environmental Biotechnology. To her surprise, she discovered a deep passion for bio-remediation research and sustainable ecology. Within two years, she led an innovative research project that developed low-cost water purification filters for rural villages, earning an international scholarship. Looking back, Priya smiled, realizing how true Helen Keller's words were: when the door of her medical career closed, a far more fulfilling door of scientific discovery had opened for her.  

5.  
**Honesty is the Best Policy**  
Raghav, a poor college student working part-time as a delivery boy, found a heavy leather wallet dropped near a park bench late one evening. Opening it to check for identification, he was stunned to find fifty thousand rupees in cash along with credit cards and an ID card belonging to a prominent businessman, Mr. Singhania.  

Though Raghav desperately needed money to pay his upcoming semester fees, his conscience refused to let him keep what was not his. He immediately rode his bicycle to Mr. Singhania's residence and handed over the wallet intact. Deeply moved by Raghav's integrity, Mr. Singhania offered to sponsor his complete higher education and hired him as a management intern in his company. Raghav's truthfulness changed his life forever, proving that honesty is indeed the best policy.
