# Picture Composition  

In a picture composition, you are asked to study the picture which is supposed to spark off an idea which could be the basis of a descriptive, or imaginative piece of writing, or even a story. The picture is likely to be a dramatic one, depicting people in action, or an event or a place.  

These are the points to remember while writing a picture composition.  
- Study the picture carefully. Pay attention to all the details such as facial expressions, gestures, clothes and posture.  
- If the composition is a story, it must have a basic plot, characters, dialogues and descriptions on the basis of the theme of the picture.  
- Always write in the past tense.  
- If the picture composition is a descriptive essay, pay attention to the details and use language that will help the reader visualise.  
- Never start by saying, 'In this picture, I see...'  
- Remember to give your composition a title.  

**Study the given pictures. Write a story or a description or an account of what each picture suggests to you. There must be a clear connection between the picture and your composition.**

# Answers – Picture Composition  


1.  
**The Courageous Rescue**  
Dark clouds gathered violently over the coastal village as crashing waves battered the wooden jetty. Old fisherman Ramu stood on the muddy shore, peering anxiously into the misty horizon where his son's small rowing boat had been caught in the sudden squall. Rain poured down relentlessly, whipping against his face, but he refused to retreat to safety. Gathering two sturdy ropes and an oarsman's lantern, Ramu rallied two brave young villagers to join him aboard a larger rescue vessel. Pushing through roaring breakers, they fought the turbulent sea until they spotted the capsized boat. With heroic effort, they pulled the exhausted young boy safely from the icy water. As night fell, the village welcomed the brave rescuers back with warm blankets and tearful relief, celebrating a father's unwavering courage.  

2.  
**Vibrancy of the Village Fair**  
The annual village fair burst into a vibrant tapestry of sights, sounds, and aromas under the bright afternoon sun. Colourful cloth canopies covered bustling stalls where artisans proudly displayed hand-painted terracotta pots, brass trinkets, and embroidered shawls. Children giggled with delight as they rode the wooden ferris wheel, their cheerful shouts mingling with the rhythmic beat of traditional folk drums. Nearby, sweet vendors prepared piping hot jalebis and fried snacks, attracting eager crowds of visitors. Farmers in crisp white turbans chatted warmly over hot cups of tea, exchanging news of the seasonal harvest. The lively fair showcased the enduring beauty of rural traditions, bringing together people of all ages in a shared spirit of joy and community celebration.  

3.  
**The Secret of the Old Mansion**  
At the end of a winding stone path stood an imposing iron gate, half-hidden under tangled ivy and overgrown wild roses. Beyond lay an ancient, abandoned mansion whose weather-worn brick walls held decades of forgotten stories. Intrigued by neighborhood rumors, young Aarav and his sister Maya pushed open the creaking gate and stepped carefully into the overgrown courtyard. Sunlight filtered through high oak trees, illuminating an elegant stone fountain surrounded by carved marble benches. In a hollow beneath an old sundial, Maya discovered a rusted metal tin containing vintage black-and-white photographs and letters from the early twentieth century. Realising they had uncovered a precious chapter of local history, the children resolved to preserve their secret sanctuary and share the historic artifacts with the town museum.
