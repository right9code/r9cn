# Notice Writing  

A notice is a brief written communication. It is written to make an announcement or to display information to a specific group of people. A notice should be presented within a box.  

**Look at this sample of a notice.**  

Notice  
MV School  
Trek to Rishikesh  

15 July 20XX  
The Adventure Club of the school is organising a three-day trek to Rishikesh in August, for students of Classes 6, 7 and 8. The total expenditure per student would be Rs 2000/-, which will include bus fare, food and lodging.  
Those interested, kindly submit your names to the undersigned by 28 July 20XX.  

Vinod Gupta  
Secretary  
Adventure Club  

The format in which a notice is drafted is very important. A notice must include—  
- the heading  
- the date when the notice is issued  
- the topic of the notice placed in a central position in line with the name of the institution  
The content of the notice should not exceed 50–55 words. It must contain the details of the event—when, where and for how long, or the last date for registration/response. Last, but not the least, there must be the name of the organisation or person issuing the notice.  

**NOTE**:  
The people who are being addressed in the notice may be mentioned below the heading, towards the left margin.  

**Now, write notices of about 50–55 words for each of these topics or situations. You can use the guidelines provided.**  
1. The students of Class 7 will be taken on a trip to the Industrial and Technological Museum on 7 December. Draft a notice informing the students that they have to bring Rs 50 for the entrance fee, bus fare and lunch.  
2. A magic show is being organised in the school in order to raise money for the disaster-stricken people of Uttarakhand. Formulate a notice informing the students of the date and venue and calling for volunteers to manage the audience.  
3. Selections for the school football team will take place on Monday, 14 August. As captain of the team, formulate a notice informing the students about the selections which will take place after school hours.  
4. A fancy dress competition is being organised by the school on Children's Day. This will give an opportunity to showcase the hidden talents of the students. As a student's coordinator, write out a notice for the same.  
5. As the editor of your school magazine, write a notice announcing an inter-class poetry writing competition on 28 September, at 3 p.m. in the library. Invite names of those interested in participating and announce that the best poems will be published in the annual edition.
