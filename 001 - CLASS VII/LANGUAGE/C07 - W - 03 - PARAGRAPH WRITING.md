# Paragraph Writing  

You are already familiar with a paragraph. Let us quickly recapitulate the characteristics of a good paragraph.  

- A paragraph has one basic idea.  
- The sentence which contains the basic idea is called the topic sentence.  
- The topic sentence usually comes at the beginning of a paragraph.  
- The topic sentence provides the base on which to build the paragraph.  
- Each paragraph has unity. Very often this is provided by the way in which sentences stand together and illustrate the main idea.  

**Let's examine this paragraph.**  

My curiosity about birds dates back to a long time. In my childhood, we had a pet mynah at home and I had taught it to pronounce over a hundred Bengali words. I believed then that the birds did not really know the meaning of what they only mechanically repeated. But I had to revise my opinion as my mynah did something amazing. One afternoon, as I was about to sit for lunch, the mynah started screeching 'Earthquake! Earthquake!' We felt nothing, but next morning, there was a report in the paper about a mild tremor. Ever since, I have maintained an interest in birds.  

Here, the topic sentence is at the beginning of the paragraph—'My curiosity about birds dates back to a long time.' The other sentences illustrate the writer's interest in birds.  
The sentences in a paragraph are interrelated. Each sentence prepares the ground for the next. A linkage is provided—  
- by **theme**—the sentences exemplify the basic idea contained in the topic sentences.  
- by **linkers** like **I, we, but**. The pronouns refer to something that has been discussed whereas the conjunctions emphasise thematic connections.  

**NOTE**:  
The sentences must follow a logical sequence to make the paragraph a coherent one!  

**A. Describe the picture in a single paragraph of about 80–100 words. Give a title to it. The following clues may help you develop the paragraph.**  
- Where is the action taking place?  
- How many people do you see in the picture? What are they doing?  
- Describe the surroundings.  
- Is there a place similar to this in your neighbourhood?  
-  Do you visit it? How often?  
- Would you like to be any of the people in the picture? Why/Why not?  

**NOTE**:  
Never start the paragraph with a sentence like, 'In this picture, I see...'. Remember, the picture is a mere guideline!

# Answers – Paragraph Writing  


**A. Description of the Picture**  

**A Lively Evening at the Local Park**  
A bustling neighborhood park comes alive as the sun begins to set. Children of all ages fill the playground, swinging high into the air, zooming down colorful slides, and playing lively games of tag across the lush green grass. Elderly residents stroll along the paved pathways or sit on wooden benches, engaged in warm conversations while enjoying the cool evening breeze. A small ice-cream vendor near the entrance attracts an eager crowd of toddlers. In my own neighborhood, there is a similar park that I visit every weekend to unwind. I would love to be one of the children playing freely on the swings, as it captures the purest joy of carefree childhood.  

**B.**  

**1.**  
It was 10 o'clock at night. I looked out of the window and noticed a shadow on the wall outside. The moon was half-hidden behind thick clouds, casting long, eerie silhouettes across the garden. My heart skipped a beat as the dark shape moved slowly toward the back porch. Quietly, I grabbed a flashlight, walked over to the door, and peeked through the blinds. To my immense relief, it was not an intruder at all, but our neighbor's friendly golden retriever, who had managed to slip past the garden gate and was looking for a midnight treat!  

**2.**  

**The great blaze that destroyed the heart of the city**  
A tragic fire broke out late yesterday evening, engulfing the historic central market area of the city in flames. Firefighters worked tirelessly for over five hours to contain the raging blaze, which destroyed dozens of heritage buildings, shops, and warehouses. The dense smoke could be seen from miles away, throwing the downtown district into utter chaos. Fortunately, prompt evacuation measures prevented any loss of life, though several firefighters sustained minor injuries. Authorities have launched an investigation to determine the exact cause of the disaster, while local community organisations are mobilizing relief supplies for affected shopkeepers.  

**Thousands stranded due to heavy rains**  
Torrential rains lashed the coastal region for the third consecutive day, leaving thousands of commuters stranded across major transit stations and flooded roadways. Public transport ground to a halt as waterlogged railway tracks forced rail authorities to suspend train services indefinitely. Rescue teams deployed inflatable boats to evacuate citizens trapped in low-lying residential sectors. Emergency shelters have been established in municipal schools to provide hot meals, clean water, and medical care to displaced families. Weather officials predict continued precipitation for the next twenty-four hours, advising residents to stay indoors until emergency crews clear the inundated roads.  

**School children decide to go green!**  
In an inspiring initiative to combat environmental degradation, students from several local schools have united to launch a massive 'Go Green' campaign across the city. The young activists organized tree-plantation drives, planting over five hundred saplings in community parks and school grounds. They also conducted door-to-door awareness rallies, educating residents on segregation of dry and wet waste, eliminating single-use plastics, and conserving electricity. Their enthusiastic efforts have received immense praise from civic leaders and citizens alike, proving that youth leadership can spark meaningful action for a sustainable and eco-friendly future.  

**3.**  

**Amit's Itinerary for the Environmental Summit**  
Amit, a dedicated student representative, is set to embark on an important journey to represent his school as an Indian ambassador at the prestigious Environmental Summit in Germany. He will depart from India on 15 July aboard a Lufthansa flight bound directly for Berlin. During his seven-day stay in the historic German capital, Amit will participate in interactive workshops, panel discussions, and collaborative youth forums addressing climate change and sustainable development. As an official delegate, he aims to present India's youth perspectives on environmental conservation while exchanging innovative ideas with international student representatives.  

**4.**  

**A Plea from the Royal Bengal Tiger**  
Standing majestically amidst the dense green foliage of the jungle, the Royal Bengal Tiger looked at me with deep, solemn eyes and spoke of the grave plight facing his species. He shared how relentless poaching, habitat destruction, and expanding human settlements have drastically reduced their forest homes and devastated their numbers over the years. Hearing the noble beast express his sorrow and fear for the future of his kin filled me with profound grief and a sharp sense of responsibility. I felt deeply moved by his words and realized the urgent need to protect wildlife habitats.
