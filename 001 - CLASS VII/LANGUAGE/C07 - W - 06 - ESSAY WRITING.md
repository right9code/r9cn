# Essay Writing  

An essay, or a composition, can be an expression of your opinions, feelings, or experiences. It is not, however, a rambling piece of writing, but a very structured form. An essay is usually divided into paragraphs. The first paragraph is called the introduction and the final paragraph is called the conclusion. The paragraphs in between the introduction and the conclusion constitute the body of the composition. These paragraphs help to develop the thoughts and ideas of the composition.  

What are the qualities of a good composition?  
- **Unity of thought**: The composition must develop one theme with a definite purpose.  
- **Order**: The composition must follow a certain ordered line of thought and come to a definite conclusion. It must not consist of haphazard reflections put down anyhow. Therefore it is of utmost importance to plan out a line of thought before beginning to write.  
- **Brevity**: A composition should not be very long. What is important is whether the composition is well written or not. Though there can be no strict rule regarding the length of a composition, one can say that it ought to be roughly around three hundred words.  
- **Style**: A composition should be written in simple and correct English. The sentence construction should be simple, direct and natural. The secret of clear writing is clear thinking. Slang, colloquial terms and free and easy constructions ought to be avoided. A composition which ‘moves’ the reader is supposed to be a good piece of writing. The words chosen ought to be justified and necessary.  
- **Personal touch**: A composition must reveal the personal feelings and opinions of the writer. Without the personal touch, the composition becomes devoid of individuality.  

How do we write a good composition?  
- **Step 1:** Think about the topic. What does it mean?  
- **Step 2:** Write down the thoughts and questions that arise in your mind after seeing the topic.  
- **Step 3:** Arrange the thoughts and questions in a logical sequence, so that one may lead to the other.  
- **Step 4:** Once the facts have been organised one can concentrate on the style. How do we make the facts appear attractive? This can be done in several ways.  
  - Start the composition with the help of a **creative and arresting introduction** by using one of the various methods:
    - by **setting the scene** of the story
    - by **plunging straight into action**
    - by an **arresting or unusual detail**
    - by a **dialogue**
  - The style of writing also includes a **meaningful conclusion**. Remember—
    - you should **round off** your narration
    - you could aim for a **surprise ending**
    - you must avoid ending your composition with sentences such as, 'In conclusion...', 'It can be seen that...' or 'Summing up, I'd like to state...'
- **Step 5:** Keep in mind the **length of the composition**. An ideal composition would be about **five paragraphs of 60 words each**. A little more or less does not make much of a difference. Remember, it is not how much you say but **what you say and how you say it**—that is what creates an impact!  
- **Step 6:** Do not forget the **personal touch**. Remember what you are writing is a composition and not an impersonal report. Therefore **your opinion and views** are most important.  

**NOTE**:  
At this point, one has to understand the difference between a descriptive composition and a narrative composition. Certain topics like 'A Village Fair' may seem a little confusing. The confusion arises in whether the composition ought to be written in the first person or in the third person. 'A Village Fair' may be written in the first person. However, the description of the fair is more important than the narrator's experience of it, which may come in as part of the conclusion! If the topic reads, 'How You Spent a Day in a Village Fair', then the narrator's experience becomes as important as the description of the fair. Remember that descriptions are a part of a narrative essay and descriptive essays may or may not be written in the first person.

# Answers – Essay Writing  


**1. Pandemonium at the Picnic**  

The winter morning was bright and crisp when our class set out for an annual picnic to a scenic lakeside resort located near the foothills. Everyone was in high spirits, singing songs on the bus ride and anticipating a peaceful day of outdoor games, delicious food, and relaxed conversation under the shaded canopy of pine trees.  

Upon arrival, we quickly set up our picnic mats, spread out our lunch boxes, and began an energetic game of badminton. However, our tranquil morning was suddenly interrupted by an unexpected visitor. A troupe of curious wild monkeys descended from the surrounding trees, attracted by the aroma of freshly cooked snacks. Within seconds, utter pandemonium broke out across the picnic grounds.  

One daring monkey snatched a container of samosas right off our table, while another grabbed our teacher's brightly colored handbag and scurried up a tall banyan tree. Students scattered in all directions, screaming in excitement and surprise, while our frantic class monitor attempted to rescue the remaining lunch baskets by covering them with picnic mats.  

Realizing that panic would only agitate the creatures, our teacher calmly suggested that we leave a tray of fruits at the base of a nearby tree. The clever tactic worked like magic; the monkeys descended to feast on the apples and bananas, dropping the stolen handbag safely onto the soft grass below. We watched from a safe distance, chuckling at the comical turn of events.  

Once the uninvited guests returned to the forest, we gathered together to tidy up the area and share whatever food remained. What began as a routine day in nature turned into an unforgettable adventure filled with laughter and excitement. Looking back, that chaotic picnic remains one of my fondest school memories, proving that unexpected surprises often make for the best stories.  

**2. Going to the Market**  

The central market in our city is a vibrant, bustling hub of activity that truly comes alive during the late afternoon hours. As one approaches the main entrance, a symphony of sounds greets the senses—hawkers shouting out enticing prices, honking rickshaws navigating narrow lanes, and the steady hum of bargaining shoppers filling the air.  

Brightly lit shops and canvas stalls line both sides of the crowded street, displaying a stunning variety of goods. Vegetable vendors arrange fresh, green produce in neat pyramids, sprinkling water over leafy spinach and crisp cucumbers to keep them looking fresh. Adjacent to them, fruit sellers showcase rows of golden mangoes, red apples, and sweet oranges, enticing passersby with their rich fragrance.  

Further down the lane, the market turns into a burst of color with textile merchants displaying traditional fabrics, sparkling bangles, and stylish garments. Shoppers move slowly from one stall to another, carefully inspecting goods and negotiating prices with seasoned shopkeepers. The atmosphere is energetic and lively, reflecting the rich cultural diversity of our local community.  

The food section of the market is particularly captivating, sending irresistible aromas of freshly fried jalebis, spicy chaat, and roasted nuts through the cool air. Families and groups of friends gather around street food carts, enjoying warm delicacies and sharing lighthearted conversations amidst the ongoing bustle of the market.  

Visiting the local market is far more than just a routine shopping trip; it is a sensory experience that captures the beating heart of our city. The dynamic energy, vibrant colors, and friendly human interactions make it a place I love to visit time and again.  

**3. An Old Favourite**  

Among the many books I have read over the years, *The Adventures of Tom Sawyer* by Mark Twain remains my absolute favourite. I first discovered this classic novel during a summer vacation, and its charm has stayed with me ever since. The story revolves around young Tom Sawyer, an imaginative and mischievous boy growing up along the banks of the Mississippi River in the nineteenth century.  

What gives me immense pleasure while reading this book is Twain's brilliant depiction of childhood innocence and adventure. Tom's clever antics—such as tricking his friends into whitewashing a fence for him by convincing them it was a privilege—are written with marvelous humor and wit. The author captures the spirit of youth so accurately that every reader can relate to Tom's desires, fears, and triumphs.  

Another feature that makes the novel so engaging is its rich background setting and memorable characters. From Tom's loyal companion Huckleberry Finn to the kind-hearted Aunt Polly, each character is portrayed with depth and authentic regional dialogue. The exciting plot moves seamlessly from lighthearted schoolroom pranks to thrilling midnight adventures in mysterious graveyards and dark caves.  

Furthermore, the book carries subtle lessons about honesty, friendship, and moral courage without ever feeling preachy. Tom's willingness to stand up for justice during critical moments shows his underlying goodness, making him a deeply endearing protagonist.  

In conclusion, *The Adventures of Tom Sawyer* is a timeless masterpiece that offers laughter, excitement, and nostalgia in equal measure. Every time I reopen its pages, I am transported back to a world of endless imagination and joyful discovery.  

**4. A Wedding in the Family**  

Last week, our entire family gathered in Jaipur to celebrate the wedding of my elder cousin, Radhika. The venue was a magnificently decorated heritage hall adorned with marigold garlands, fairy lights, and elegant silk drapes. The air was filled with festive excitement as relatives from distant cities arrived to participate in the traditional ceremonies.  

The wedding festivities spanned three memorable days, beginning with the lively Mehendi and Sangeet functions. Family members of all ages performed traditional dances and sang folk songs, creating a joyful environment. On the main wedding day, the groom arrived in a grand procession accompanied by lively brass band music and dancing guests. The central wedding ritual took place around a sacred fire under a beautifully decorated mandap, where solemn vows were recited by the couple.  

What I liked most about the event was the warm sense of togetherness that brought our extended family close. Reconnecting with cousins, sharing old stories, and participating in rich cultural rituals made the occasion truly special. Additionally, the grand wedding feast—featuring a magnificent spread of traditional Rajasthani dishes and sweets—was an absolute delight for everyone present.  

However, there were a few aspects of the event that I found slightly unpleasant. The continuous use of loud sound systems late into the night was exhausting, especially for senior family members. Furthermore, I observed a considerable amount of food wastage at the buffet counters, which could have been avoided with better management.  

Despite these minor drawbacks, my cousin's wedding was a beautiful celebration of love, culture, and family bonds. The vibrant memories of colorful attire, joyous laughter, and solemn customs will remain etched in my heart for years to come.  

**5. My Favourite Extra-curricular Activities**  

In today's fast-paced academic environment, extra-curricular activities provide a vital outlet for creativity and self-expression. In my spare time, I love engaging in two main activities: playing classical violin and participating in school debate competitions. Both pursuits bring immense joy to my life while shaping my personality in unique ways.  

Practicing the violin requires patience, discipline, and consistent effort. Learning to read musical notation and controlling the bow to produce sweet melodies demands hours of focused concentration. When I play music, I feel a sense of inner calm and emotional freedom that helps me recharge after long hours of study. Music has taught me that mastery over any skill requires steady perseverance and dedication.  

On the other hand, participating in debate competitions challenges me to think critically and express my ideas with clarity and confidence. Preparing arguments on complex contemporary issues sharpens my analytical skills and encourages me to view problems from multiple perspectives. Standing on stage before a large audience has helped me overcome stage fright and develop strong public speaking skills.  

Together, these extra-curricular pursuits contribute significantly to my overall character development. Music nurtures my emotional intelligence and instills patience, while debating fosters mental agility, confidence, and articulate communication. They have taught me how to balance discipline with creativity and work gracefully both individually and in team settings.  

In conclusion, extra-curricular activities are essential for holistic personal growth. They enrich our school life, build essential life skills, and help us discover our true passions beyond textbooks and examinations.
