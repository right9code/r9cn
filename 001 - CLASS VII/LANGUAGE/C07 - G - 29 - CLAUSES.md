# Clauses  

**Identify the subject and the verb in each group of words.**  
- although he is a lawyer in the high court  
- but it is really surprising  
- but she did it anyway  
- where I jog  
- that I want a bike  
- because I like it  

These groups of words have a subject and a verb. But are these sentences?  
No, they are not sentences because a sentence is a group of words which contains at least one finite verb and its subject and also makes complete sense. These groups of words do not make complete sense and are dependent on some other word or group of words to do so.  

A group of words that contains a finite verb and has a subject but forms a part of a sentence is called a **clause**.  

Now, read these sentences and notice the highlighted groups of words.  
- Rita sat **on a wooden chair**.  
- They bought this chair **because it was cosy**.  

The highlighted group of words in the first sentence does not have a subject or a verb. So it is a phrase.  

The highlighted group of words in the second sentence has a subject and a verb, but it does not make complete sense, so it is a clause.  

Some clauses may not have subjects, but they will always have a finite verb. For example:  

- Rita sat on a chair **that was made of wood**.  
- Mamta came first in the class, **which surprised her friends**.  

Did you know that each of the above sentences can be rewritten as two sentences? Let us see how.  
- Rita sat on a chair. It was made of wood.  
- Mamta came first in the class. It surprised her friends.  

**NOTE**:  
Every sentence is also a clause.  

Study this table to understand the difference between a phrase, a clause and a sentence.  

| Phrase | Clause | Sentence |  
| --- | --- | --- |  
| A phrase is a meaningful group of words. | A clause is a meaningful group of words. | A sentence is a meaningful group of words. |  
| It does not make complete sense by itself. | It may or may not be dependent on something else for complete meaning. | It expresses a complete thought. |  
| It has no subject or predicate. | It has a subject and a predicate. | It has a subject and a predicate. |  
| It has no finite verb. | It has a finite verb. | It has a finite verb. |
